原住民族（含部落） Indigenous peoples (including tribes/communities)

台灣法律上的用語，由原住民族基本法定義；平台的「原住民族文化」議題指涉相關案例。

說明

《原住民族基本法》第 2 條定義：原住民族是「既存於臺灣而為國家管轄內之傳統民族」，包括阿美族、泰雅族、排灣族、布農族、卑南族、魯凱族、鄒族、賽夏族、雅美族、邵族、噶瑪蘭族、太魯閣族及其他經行政院核定者；「部落」是原住民於原住民族地區一定區域內，依傳統規範共同生活結合而成、經中央主管機關核定的團體（本詞條讀的是臺北市法規查詢系統轉載的版本，法規頁面標示最近修正日期為民國 107 年 6 月 20 日；請以全國法規資料庫現行條文為準，平台沒有逐條比對是否有更新）。

平台的「原住民族文化」議題，是依案例來源有沒有談到原住民族的文化、語言、土地或族人參與來標示，不是法定身分的認定。案例的族群、部落名稱請以來源為準。

Definition (English)

A legal term defined by Taiwan's Indigenous Peoples Basic Law; the platform's "Indigenous culture" issue marks related cases.

Article 2 of the Indigenous Peoples Basic Law defines Indigenous peoples as traditional peoples existing in Taiwan within the state's jurisdiction, listing the Amis, Atayal, Paiwan, Bunun, Puyuma, Rukai, Tsou, Saisiyat, Yami, Thao, Kavalan, Truku and others recognised by the Executive Yuan; a "tribe" is a group of Indigenous people living together by traditional norms in an Indigenous area and approved by the central authority. The text read is a version reproduced by the Taipei City legal database, showing a last amendment of 20 June 2018; check the current text on the national database, as the platform did not compare for later changes.

The platform's "Indigenous culture" issue tag follows whether a source discusses Indigenous culture, language, land or participation; it is not a determination of legal status. Names of peoples and tribes follow each source.
