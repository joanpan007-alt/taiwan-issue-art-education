做十六歲 Coming-of-age at sixteen

台南府城特有的成年禮：十六歲向七娘媽祭拜，象徵轉大人。

說明

『做十六歲』是府城舊城區特有的成年禮，傳承自清領時期的五條港：當時港區童工薪資只有成年人的一半，滿十六歲才能領全薪，家人多設宴款待工頭與親友。成年禮由十六歲青少年向七娘媽祭拜，台南主祀七娘媽的廟宇以開隆宮、臨水夫人廟為主、儀式多在開隆宮舉辦，後來在市府推廣下逐漸成為全市活動。

平台案例中，十六歲正青春藝術節（K50）正是把這個在地成年禮傳統，轉化為每年暑假的青少年藝術節與集體創作劇場。

Definition (English)

Tainan's distinctive coming-of-age rite: at sixteen, youths give thanks to the Seven Goddesses, marking adulthood.

Per CNA, 'doing sixteen' is a coming-of-age rite distinctive to the old city of Tainan, handed down from the Qing-era Wutiaogang port district: child workers there earned half an adult's wage and could draw a full wage only at sixteen, so families would hold a banquet for the foreman and relatives. At the rite sixteen-year-olds give thanks to the Seven Goddesses (Qiniangma); the main temples are Kailong Temple and Linshui Furen Temple, with the rite mostly held at Kailong Temple, and the city government later grew it into a city-wide event.

In the platform's cases, the Sixteen Coming-of-Age Youth Arts Festival (K50) turns this local rite into an annual summer youth arts festival and collective-creation theatre. This entry follows CNA.
