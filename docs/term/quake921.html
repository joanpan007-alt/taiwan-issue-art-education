九二一地震 921 earthquake

1999 年 9 月 21 日發生在台灣中部的大地震。

說明

國立自然科學博物館的 921 地震教育園區簡介寫道，地震發生在 1999 年 9 月 21 日凌晨 1 點 47 分，芮氏規模達到 7.3，造成 2400 多人死亡，上萬棟房屋倒塌。農業部農村發展及水土保持署的歷史影像平台另補充：震央在南投縣集集鎮（故又稱「集集大地震」），肇因為車籠埔斷層錯動，地震持續約 102 秒；死亡 2454 人、失蹤 29 人、受傷超過 1 萬人，房屋全倒 51,711 棟、半倒 53,768 棟（該頁數據為平台改寫自維基百科與重建計畫的整理，非原始官方統計）。

平台的案例中，「畫，25」九二一震後埔里展（K30）在地震 25 週年時，邀畫家回到埔里受災地創作；石岡媽媽劇團（K51）則是 921 後差事劇團在石岡、東勢災區辦民眾戲劇工作坊，由受災的客家婦女組成、延續至今的社區劇團。

學理來源

  1. 簡介－921 地震教育園區（國立自然科學博物館）
  2. 921大地震（集集大地震）歷史影像平台（農業部農村發展及水土保持署）

相關案例（2）

「畫，25」九二一震後埔里展石岡媽媽劇團

Definition (English)

The major earthquake that struck central Taiwan on September 21, 1999.

The 921 Earthquake Museum of Taiwan's introduction, run by the National Museum of Natural Science, says the earthquake occurred at 1:47 a.m. on September 21, 1999, with Richter magnitude 7.3, killing over 2,400 people and collapsing tens of thousands of buildings. The historical-image platform of the Ministry of Agriculture's Rural Development and Soil and Water Conservation Agency adds that the epicentre was in Jiji, Nantou (hence "Jiji earthquake"), caused by rupture of the Chelungpu fault and lasting about 102 seconds, with 2,454 dead, 29 missing and over 10,000 injured, and 51,711 buildings fully and 53,768 partly collapsed (these figures are that platform's compilation from Wikipedia and reconstruction plans, not original official statistics).

Among the platform's cases, "Hua, 25" Puli exhibition (K30) invites painters back to the quake-hit area of Puli on the 25th anniversary, and Shigang Mama Theatre (K51) is a community troupe of quake-affected Hakka women, mentored by Assignment Theatre through post-921 People's Theatre workshops in Shigang and Dongshi and still active today.
