名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

社會參與式藝術 Socially engaged art

定義：把人與社群的實際參與納為創作一部分、以協作與互動為方法的藝術實踐，過程往往與成品同等重要。

說明

Tate 的藝術名詞表說明，這類實踐具有協作性，常帶有參與性，並把「人」當作作品的媒材。任何涉及人與社群進行討論、合作或社交互動的藝術形式都可以納入。共同創作的過程有時和作品本身一樣重要，藝術家也常需要長時間融入社群。

在台灣，有研究指出這類實踐是 1990 年代後期以來的趨勢，與後解嚴時期的在地認同、社區總體營造相呼應。另一篇研究提醒，常見的「藝術介入」一詞與它不完全相同，社會參與式藝術更強調人與社群之間的討論與互動。

在藝術教育的脈絡裡，社會參與式藝術也發展成一套取向。Chung 與 Li（2020）引用 Schlemmer 等人的定義，指社會參與式的藝術活動『超越製作賞心悅目的作品，轉而促成一種整合藝術實踐、教學歷程與創造可能、以追求更公平世界的對話』；它常與『社會正義藝術教育』（social justice art education）並提——鼓勵學生從自身的身分與經驗出發，調查不公義的社會處境、以創作想像更公平的社會。該文以小學生透過街頭藝術家 Skid Robot 的作品認識無家者議題、再以版畫為無家者『畫一個家』為例。

Pablo Helguera 在《社會參與藝術的十個關鍵概念》（吳岱融、蘇瑤華譯，北藝大，2018）第一章「定義」提出一個「暫時性的定義」：所有藝術都是為與他人溝通而造、因此都是「社會的」，但社會參與藝術的關鍵在於真實的「社會交往」（social intercourse）。他區分「象徵的」（symbolic）與「真實的」（actual）實踐——社會參與藝術建立在真實（而非想像或假定）的社會行動之上，並援引哈伯馬斯（Jürgen Habermas）《溝通行動理論》（1981）的「溝通行動」為據；他也把「關係美學」「社區藝術」「合作式藝術」「參與式藝術」「對話式藝術」「公共藝術」列為這一波實踐的多個別名。

Definition (English)

Definition: Art practice that treats the real participation of people and communities as part of the work, through collaboration and interaction, where process often matters as much as the result.

Tate's art terms entry says this kind of practice is collaborative, often participatory, and involves people as the medium or material of the work. Any art form that involves discussion, collaboration or social interaction with people and communities can fall under it. Making together can matter as much as the work itself, and artists often need to be embedded in a community for a long time.

In Taiwan, one study describes it as a trend since the late 1990s, resonating with post-martial-law local identity and community building. Another study cautions that the common term "art intervention" is not quite the same: socially engaged art puts more emphasis on discussion and interaction between people and communities.

In art education, socially engaged art has also grown into a teaching approach. Chung and Li (2020), citing Schlemmer and colleagues, describe socially engaged art activities as ones that 'stretch beyond the production of aesthetically pleasing art objects to foster a dialog that integrates artistic practices, pedagogical processes, and creative possibilities in pursuit of a more equitable world'; it is often paired with 'social justice art education', which encourages students to start from their own identities and experiences, investigate unjust social conditions and imagine a fairer society through art. Their article has elementary students learn about homelessness through the street artist Skid Robot's work and then 'draw a home' for homeless people through printmaking.

In Education for Socially Engaged Art (Chinese ed. trans. Wu Dai-Rong and Su Yao-Hua, TNUA, 2018), Chapter 1 'Definition', Pablo Helguera offers a 'provisional definition': all art is made to communicate and is therefore 'social', but the key to socially engaged art is actual 'social intercourse'. He distinguishes 'symbolic' from 'actual' practice - socially engaged art rests on actual (not imagined or assumed) social action - and draws on Jurgen Habermas's communicative action (The Theory of Communicative Action, 1981); he also lists relational aesthetics, community art, collaborative art, participatory art, dialogic art and public art as several names for this wave of practice.
