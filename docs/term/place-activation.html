名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

地域策展／閒置空間活化 Place-based curation / activating idle spaces

定義：以一塊地域、或一處閒置、廢棄的空間為對象的藝術工作——從田野踏查、紀錄、個人創作，到用策展把空間重新組織、活化。

說明

這是平台為了收攏一組相關實踐而採用的工作概念。它和既有的『策展』『限地製作（場域特定藝術）』『社區營造』相鄰：差別在於它以『一個地方／一處閒置空間』為單位，把調查、徵集或共創、展呈串成一條線，目標是讓空間本身被重新看見、甚至在計畫結束後仍持續被使用。

要留意的是，被歸在這個概念下的案例其實落在一條光譜的不同位置：有的以『踏查與檔案』盤點閒置空間（如 k70《海市蜃樓：台灣閒置公共設施抽樣踏查計劃》對全台蚊子館的抽樣踏查），有的是藝術家在廢墟現場的個人創作（如 k71《廢墟影像晶體計畫》），有的才是嚴格意義的『地域策展與活化』（如 k78 星濱山在基隆正濱漁港以 CIR 與策展活化老屋與港町）。引用時宜分清：踏查紀錄、個人創作、與共創活化是不同程度的介入，不宜一概稱為『活化』。

與操作卡『在歷史現場與軍事遺構裡創作（site）』也不同：那談的是在單一現場做一件作品，這裡談的是用策展把整個空間或地域組織、活化起來。

Definition (English)

Definition: art work that takes a place, or an idle or abandoned space, as its object — from fieldwork and documentation, to individual creation on site, to using curation to re-organise and reactivate the space.

This is a working concept the platform uses to gather a set of related practices. It sits next to ‘curation’, ‘site-specific art’ and ‘community building’: the difference is that it takes ‘a place / an idle space’ as its unit, stringing survey, commissioning or co-creation, and display into one line, so that the space itself is seen anew and, ideally, keeps being used after the project ends.

Note that the cases placed under this concept sit at different points on a spectrum: some survey and archive idle spaces (e.g. k70, a sampling survey of Taiwan’s ‘mosquito halls’ — unused public facilities); some are an artist’s individual creation in ruins (e.g. k71, The Ruin Image Crystal Project); and only some are place-based curation and activation in the strict sense (e.g. k78, Xingbinshan reactivating old buildings and the port town of Zhengbin through CIR and curation). When citing, keep these apart: documentation, individual creation and co-creative activation are different degrees of intervention and should not all be called ‘activation’.

It also differs from the method card ‘working inside historical sites and military remains (site)’: that is about making one work in a single place, whereas this is about using curation to organise and reactivate a whole space or locality.
