名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

藝術教育 Arts education

定義：透過藝術的學習與創作培養美感、表達與理解能力的教育，常在學校或社區進行。

說明

教育部在政策上較常用「美感教育」，並說明它是培育美感素養的實踐，引導學習者覺察、探索、感受、認識並實踐美。該計畫文件沒有單獨定義「藝術教育」，但引用 2019 年世界藝術教育聯盟的宣言，認為藝術教育是促進社會文化永續發展的重要支柱。

平台裡，以學生或學習者為主要參與者、並以學習和美感培養為目的的計畫，會標為藝術教育，例如國中生參與的官田有妖怪、玫好時光。

學理來源

  1. 教育部《美感教育中長程計畫第三期五年計畫（113–117 年）》（民國 112 年 9 月，由學校網站轉載）
  2. 廖敦如〈社區取向的藝術教育——博物館與學校合作之統整課程設計〉，《美育》第 135 期（國立臺灣藝術教育館）

Definition (English)

Definition: Education that cultivates aesthetic sense, expression and understanding through learning and making art, often in schools or communities.

The Ministry of Education more often uses "aesthetic education" and describes it as practice that cultivates aesthetic literacy, guiding learners to notice, explore, feel, know and practice beauty. The plan does not define "arts education" on its own, but quotes a 2019 declaration of the World Alliance for Arts Education that arts education is an important pillar for sustainable cultural development.

On this platform, projects whose main participants are students or learners and whose purpose includes learning and aesthetic development are labeled arts education, such as Guantian Has Monsters with junior high students and the Good Times podcast.
