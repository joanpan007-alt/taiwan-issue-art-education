名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

教育性藝術計畫 Pedagogical art project

定義：把教學與教育過程本身當作創作媒介與作品形式的藝術計畫。

說明

在平台的案例中，義家藝館是例子。GPI 政府出版品資訊網 2022 年的書評，把吳岱融的《當教學成為形式》稱為「一個教育性藝術計畫的生成與反響」，書評說這個計畫由北藝大藝教所與臺北榮譽國民之家合作，參與者包括修課學生、藝術家教師與榮家的義士居民及行政團隊；計畫團隊網站也寫明這是搭配選修課程的教學暨創作計畫。

國際上有相近的討論。Paul O'Neill 與 Mick Wilson 編的《Curating and the Educational Turn》出版公告，把近年將教學法納入藝術與策展實踐的現象，描述為藝術朝向教育的典範性轉向。Claire Bishop《Artificial Hells》的書籍介紹也說，全書最後討論當代藝術家的長期教育計畫，並舉 Thomas Hirschhorn、Tania Bruguera、Pawel Althamer 與 Paul Chan 為例。

平台提醒：沒有查到中文或英文來源，為「教育性藝術計畫」或 pedagogical art project 給出正式定義；這個詞是編輯整理，英文也是平台的對應譯名，沒有來源逐字使用。它和「藝術教育」的區分也是平台的判斷：藝術教育以學習與美感培養為目的，教育性藝術計畫則把教學過程當作作品的一部分；兩者在實務上常重疊，義家藝館既是課程也是創作。

Definition (English)

Definition: An art project that treats teaching and the educational process itself as its medium and form.

Among the platform's cases, Yijia Art Museum is an example. A 2022 review on the GPI government publications site calls Wu Dai-rong's book When Teaching Becomes Form the story of "an educational art project"; it says the project is a partnership between the Graduate Institute of Arts and Humanities Education at Taipei National University of the Arts and the Taipei Honor Home for Veterans, with participants including enrolled students, artist-teachers, and the veteran residents and administrative staff of the home. The team's own website says it is a teaching-and-creating project tied to an elective course.

There are comparable discussions internationally. The announcement of Curating and the Educational Turn, edited by Paul O'Neill and Mick Wilson, describes the recent incorporation of pedagogy into art and curatorial practice as art's paradigmatic re-orientation towards the educational. The description of Claire Bishop's Artificial Hells says the book ends with long-term educational projects by contemporary artists, citing Thomas Hirschhorn, Tania Bruguera, Pawel Althamer and Paul Chan.

Platform note: no Chinese or English source was found that formally defines this term or "pedagogical art project"; the entry is an editorial summary, and the English is the platform's own rendering, not a phrase used verbatim by a source. The distinction from arts education is also the platform's judgment: arts education aims at learning and cultivating aesthetic sensibility, while a pedagogical art project treats the teaching process as part of the work; in practice they often overlap, and Yijia Art Museum is both a course and a creative project.
