名詞解釋 ／ 議題與社會背景

地方記憶 Local memory

居民對所在地方的共同回憶：景象、故事與生活方式。

說明

地方記憶指居民對所在地方的共同回憶，包括老街的景象、已消失的建築、生活習慣和故事。許多計畫以蒐集或重現這些記憶為起點，例如岡山居民製作記憶箱子、街事美術館請長者採集回憶。

這是平台最常使用的議題標籤。它是編輯整理的用詞，沒有單一的學術來源；常用的方法包括口述歷史與田野調查。

Definition (English)

Residents' shared recollections of a place: its scenes, stories and ways of life.

Local memory means residents' shared recollections of where they live, including the look of old streets, vanished buildings, habits and stories. Many projects start by collecting or recreating these memories, such as Gangshan residents making memory boxes and the Street Museum asking elders to collect recollections.

This is the platform's most commonly used issue tag. It is an editorial term with no single scholarly source; common methods include oral history and fieldwork.
