名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

限地製作（場域特定藝術） Site-specific art

為某個特定地點量身創作、與該地點不可分離的藝術；移到別處意義就會改變。

說明

ArtLex 把限地製作（site-specific art）定義為「為精確地點構思、並與該地點互動的藝術介入」，作品的意義與那個地方綁在一起。它的『地點』可以是城市、鄉野，也可以是美術館或畫廊內部，涵蓋地景藝術（如 Smithson《螺旋堤》）、為展間而作的裝置（如 Turrell、Flavin），以及公共空間的委託創作（如 Serra《傾斜之弧》）。換句話說，限地製作並不限定場地必須是歷史或軍事場域。

平台的案例中，義家藝館（K37）、馬祖國際藝術島（K46）、眾人成莊（K44）、二村開門（K45）都把特定場域（榮民之家、戰地據點、眷村遺構）當成作品不可分割的一部分。教學方法頁的「在歷史現場與軍事遺構裡創作」，可看成限地製作用在記憶場域教學的一種應用，而不是另一種形式。

Definition (English)

Art made for one particular place and inseparable from it; move it elsewhere and the meaning changes.

ArtLex defines site-specific art as "a type of artistic intervention specifically conceived for a precise location and that interacts with it", its meaning tied to that place. The site can be urban, rural, or inside a museum or gallery, and the term covers land art (e.g. Smithson's Spiral Jetty), installations made for a gallery (e.g. Turrell, Flavin) and public commissions (e.g. Serra's Tilted Arc). So site-specific work does not require a historical or military setting.

Among the platform's cases, the Veterans' Home art museum (K37), Matsu International Art Island (K46), Making the Village Together (K44) and Second Village Opens (K45) all treat a particular site as an inseparable part of the work. The teaching method 'Working inside historical sites and military remains' is one educational application of site-specificity to memory-laden places, not a separate art form.
