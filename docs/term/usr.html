名詞解釋 ／ 資料與計畫用語

大學社會責任（USR）與社會實踐計畫 University Social Responsibility (USR) and Social-Practice Programs

政府鼓勵大學把師生帶進社區、解決地方問題的計畫；USR 為教育部（高教深耕的一環），另有國科會系統的『人文創新與社會實踐（HISP）』。

說明

教育部主導的大學社會責任實踐（USR）計畫在 2017 年試辦、2018 年啟動第一期，做法包括師生進入社區駐點，與社會團體及民眾共同盤點地方現況、研擬解決方法。依教育部 USR 推動中心的成果電子書，第一期（2018–2019）114 所大專校院、220 件計畫，第二期（2020–2022）約 93 校、204 件，第三期（2023–2024）113 校、251 件，並逐期接軌聯合國永續發展目標（SDGs）與校務治理。

USR 的時程與教育部『高等教育深耕計畫（高教深耕）』相互配合，常被歸在高教深耕『善盡社會責任』的面向之下。平台案例中，打開岡山·記憶的箱子（K01）由中山大學管理學院 USR 計畫團隊執行；官田有妖怪（K31）屬南藝大在官田的 USR 計畫（藝術史學系執行、官田國中協辦），南藝大電子報與自由時報亦以『高教深耕善盡社會責任』描述。義家藝館（K37）出現在北藝大高教深耕『善盡社會責任』的成果脈絡裡，但依深耕辦公室頁面屬『校級扶植』、經費來自主冊，並非正式 USR 子計畫，故本平台不將它列為 USR 案例。

另一個相近但不同部會的計畫，是科技部（今國科會）人文司的『人文創新與社會實踐（HISP）計畫』，約自 2013 年起推動，以人文社會研究連結地方、回應區域社會變遷，線上平台為『新作坊』（hisp.ntu.edu.tw）。平台案例中，《潮濕的複寫本》街區藝術行動（K29）即由國立臺灣海洋大學的 HISP 計畫執行——與 USR 精神相近，但屬國科會系統、不是教育部 USR。

Definition (English)

Government programs encouraging universities to bring teachers and students into communities to tackle local problems; USR is run by the Ministry of Education (part of the Higher Education Sprout Project), alongside the National Science and Technology Council's Humanities Innovation and Social Practice (HISP).

The Ministry of Education's University Social Responsibility (USR) program began as a pilot in 2017 and launched its first phase in 2018, with teachers and students stationed in communities to survey local conditions and devise solutions with social groups and residents. Per the USR Office's results e-book, the first phase (2018–2019) had 114 institutions and 220 projects, the second (2020–2022) about 93 institutions and 204 projects, and the third (2023–2024) 113 institutions and 251 projects, increasingly aligned with the UN SDGs and campus governance.

USR runs in step with the Ministry's Higher Education Sprout Project and is usually placed under the Project's "social responsibility" strand. Among the platform's cases, the Gangshan memory boxes (K01) are run by the USR team at National Sun Yat-sen University's College of Management; Guantian Has Monsters (K31) belongs to TNNUA's USR project in Guantian (run by the Art History Department, co-hosted with Guantian Junior High), which the university e-newsletter and Liberty Times describe under the Sprout Project's social-responsibility strand. Yijia Art Museum (K37) appears in TNUA's Sprout-Project "social responsibility" results, but per the office's page it is a campus-level supported item funded from the main grant, not a formal USR sub-project, so the platform does not list it as a USR case.

A kindred but differently-administered program is the National Science and Technology Council (formerly Ministry of Science and Technology) Humanities Innovation and Social Practice (HISP) program, running since around 2013, which links humanities and social-science research to localities and responds to regional social change through its "SOWROW" platform (hisp.ntu.edu.tw). Among the cases, the Damp Palimpsest neighbourhood art action (K29) is run by National Taiwan Ocean University's HISP project — close in spirit to USR, but under the NSTC system, not the Ministry of Education's USR.
