浪漫台三線 Romantic Route 3

2017 年起推動的客庄地區建設與文化活動計畫，並辦有浪漫台三線藝術季。

說明

行政院 2023 年的新聞稿寫道，蔡英文總統上任後，於 2017 年推動「浪漫台三線計畫」，以 4 年 20 億元推動；計畫在「台三線、六堆、台九線等地區」做基礎建設，之後接續「客庄 369 幸福計畫」（2020 年編列 25 億元）。政策目標是用新的概念推動客家文化活動，重新定義客家傳統。同一份新聞稿提到，當年的藝術季於 2023 年 6 月 24 日至 8 月 27 日舉行，以地景藝術與客家美食帶動客庄旅遊與地方創生。

平台的案例中，浪漫台三線藝術季（K22）是這個計畫的一部分。目前只有行政院這一份來源，頁面沒有說明台三線這條路本身的範圍與涵蓋縣市，平台也不另外補充。

2019 浪漫台三線藝術季（首屆）「有的是時間」

Definition (English)

A programme begun in 2017 for infrastructure and cultural activities in Hakka areas, which also runs the Romantic Route 3 Art Festival.

A 2023 Executive Yuan press release says that after taking office President Tsai Ing-wen launched the "Romantic Route 3 programme" in 2017 with NT$2 billion over four years; it built infrastructure in "Route 3, the Liudui area, Route 9 and other areas," followed by the "Hakka Village 369 Happiness programme" (NT$2.5 billion budgeted in 2020). The policy goal is to promote Hakka cultural activities with new concepts and redefine Hakka tradition. The same release says that year's art festival ran from June 24 to August 27, 2023, using land art and Hakka food to drive Hakka-village tourism and local revitalisation.

Among the platform's cases, the Romantic Route 3 Art Festival (K22) is part of this programme. The Executive Yuan release is the only source so far; it does not describe the extent of Route 3 itself or the counties it covers, and the platform does not add that.
