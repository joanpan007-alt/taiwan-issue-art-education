名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

策展 Curation

定義：規劃一檔展覽的工作：訂定主題、挑選作品、安排詮釋與呈現方式。

說明

ART.ZIP 的文章指出，curator 一詞源自拉丁語 cura，原意是照料與保管，後來才指負責藏品保管、研究並策劃展覽的人。當代藝術裡，策展人的工作包括展覽策劃、預算與日程管理、借展洽談、研究與寫作，並被比喻為連結藝術界與大眾的「翻譯」。

平台把以展覽形式呈現、由策展團隊規劃的計畫標為「策展與展覽」。官田有妖怪就是一例，自由時報稱之為「策展」，由南藝大藝術史系團隊規劃。

Definition (English)

Definition: The work of planning an exhibition - setting its theme, selecting works and arranging how they are interpreted and shown.

An ART.ZIP article notes that "curator" comes from the Latin cura, meaning care, and later came to mean the person who looks after collections, researches and plans exhibitions. In contemporary art, a curator's work includes planning exhibitions, managing budgets and schedules, negotiating loans, research and writing, and curators have been likened to "translators" between the art world and the public.

The platform labels projects presented as exhibitions and planned by a curatorial team as "curation & exhibitions." Guantian Has Monsters is one example: the Liberty Times calls it a curated show planned by a team from the art history department at Tainan National University of the Arts.
