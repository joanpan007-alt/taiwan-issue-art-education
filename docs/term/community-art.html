名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

社區藝術 Community art

定義：在特定社區中進行、與居民互動或共同完成的藝術活動。

說明

Tate 把社群藝術（community art）描述為在社群環境中進行、強調與社群互動或對話的藝術活動。它被列為社會參與式實踐的相關概念，但不是同一個詞。

在台灣，社區藝術常和「社區營造」一起被提起。社區營造是文建會自 1994 年推行的政策，期望居民共同解決生活問題、加強彼此連結，文化與藝術在其中扮演重要角色。平台把以社區居民為主要參與者的計畫標為社區藝術。

Definition (English)

Definition: Art activities carried out within a particular community, interacting with or made together with its residents.

Tate describes community art as art activity based in a community setting and designed to interact or engage with the community. It lists it as a concept related to, but not the same as, socially engaged practice.

In Taiwan, community art is often mentioned together with "community building," a policy promoted by the Council for Cultural Affairs from 1994 that hopes residents will solve everyday problems together and strengthen ties, with culture and art playing an important role. The platform labels projects whose main participants are community residents as community art.
