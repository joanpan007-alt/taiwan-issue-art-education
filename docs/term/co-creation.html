名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

共創／共同創作 Co-creation

藝術家與參與者一起決定並完成作品，而不是單方面創作。

說明

共創指作品由藝術家和參與者共同發想、製作，甚至共同決定方向。參與的程度可以差很多：有的只是請居民提供物件或故事，有的讓居民參與決策。

平台提醒，報導寫「居民參與」不一定等於「共同決策」。這也是為什麼部分案例的備註會寫「居民在決策上的角色，報導沒有說明」。

Definition (English)

Artists and participants decide on and complete the work together, rather than the artist creating alone.

Co-creation means the work is conceived and made by the artist and participants together, sometimes with a shared say in direction. The degree varies a lot: in some projects residents merely provide objects or stories, in others they take part in decisions.

The platform reminds readers that a report saying "residents took part" does not necessarily mean "shared decision-making." That is why some case notes say the residents' role in decisions is not stated in the report.
