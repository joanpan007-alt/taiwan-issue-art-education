共食共作：以餐桌與食物凝聚關係

先不急著創作，用一起吃飯、交換食譜、共同備餐等飲食活動當媒介，讓參與者先建立關係與信任，關係成熟後再長出作品。

怎麼帶

  1. 固定節奏的共餐或共同備餐（例如每月一次『一家一菜』）
  2. 用食物交換引出個人故事：換食譜就是換記憶、換家鄉
  3. 先累積關係與信任，不要第一次就談創作
  4. 關係成熟後再導入共創，並把餐桌經驗轉成作品或刊物

適用

社宅、社區、長照與跨文化等『需要先交陪』的場域；對陌生、異質或流動的住民特別有效。

Method (English)

Cooking and eating together

Don’t rush to make art; use shared meals, recipe-swaps and cooking together as the medium, letting participants build relationships and trust first, and let the work grow once the relationships are there.

  1. Hold regular shared meals or cook-togethers (e.g. a monthly potluck)
  2. Use food exchange to draw out personal stories: swapping recipes swaps memories and homelands
  3. Build relationship and trust first — don’t talk about ‘creating’ at the first meal
  4. Bring in co-creation once the relationships are there, and turn the table into a work or a zine

Social housing, communities, elder care and cross-cultural settings that need trust first; especially good for unfamiliar, mixed or transient residents.
