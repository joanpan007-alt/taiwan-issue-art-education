計畫簡介

馬祖國際藝術島第 2 屆於 2023 年以「生紅過夏」為主題（取馬祖釀老酒的俗諺），延續島內社區與常民之間的關係，自 9 月下旬起於連江四鄉五島展開。

亮點包括首次對外開放的北竿秘密電廠，藉光影與音像重現馬祖作為戰地前線的歷史。

2023 Matsu Biennial (2nd ed.) — 'Reddening Through Summer'

The 2nd Matsu International Art Island, themed 'Reddening Through Summer' (from a Matsu liquor-brewing saying), ran from late September 2023 across the four townships and five islands, continuing ties between island communities and everyday life. Highlights included the Beigan secret power plant, opened to the public for the first time, using light and sound to re-present Matsu's front-line history.
