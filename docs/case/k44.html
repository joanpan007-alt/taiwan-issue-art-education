計畫簡介

《眾人成莊：中興莊共景行動》是彰化縣文化局為中興莊眷村文化園區公共藝術設置所辦的民眾參與計畫，展覽 2026 年 5 月 2 日至 31 日，藝術行動在 5 月 2、3、9、10 日。

安排一系列讓民眾動手的行動：聲音採集與卡牌遊戲工作坊、木構搭建、親子互動劇場、VR 創作與攝影。

目標是把原本封閉的眷村遺址轉為開放的公共場域，讓外界與居民一起重新看見這裡的生活記憶。來源寫到活動日期、工作坊類型、藝術家與主辦單位，但沒有寫實際參與人數或成果。

操作手法

  • 用聲音採集與卡牌遊戲工作坊，讓民眾動手重新看見眷村的生活記憶。

Summary (English)

Making the Village Together: Zhongxing New Village Co-scene Action

“Everyone Makes a Village: Zhongxing Village Shared-Scenery Action” is a public participation programme run by the Changhua County Cultural Affairs Bureau for the public art installation at the Zhongxing Village military dependents’ village cultural park; the exhibition runs from 2 to 31 May 2026, with art actions on 2, 3, 9 and 10 May.

It arranges a series of hands-on actions: sound collection and card-game workshops, timber-frame building, parent-child interactive theatre, VR creation and photography.

The aim is to turn a formerly closed dependents’ village site into an open public space and let outsiders and residents rediscover its living memories together. The sources give dates, workshop types, artists and organisers, but not actual participant numbers or results.
