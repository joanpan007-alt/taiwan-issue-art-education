新聞稿 Press release

由主辦或執行單位撰寫、提供給媒體的稿件；多篇報導用語相近時，常是同一份新聞稿的轉載。

說明

這是平台的編輯說明，沒有引用定義來源。平台在判斷案例的「已查證」時，把用語高度相近、疑似同源的報導視為同一份來源，不算獨立來源，例如 K32 的三篇報導。

這個判斷是平台的規則，不代表報導不可信：新聞稿本身可能是正確的，但無法互相印證。可參考「業配文」詞條，兩者性質不同：新聞稿是單位提供稿件，業配文則是付費內容。

Definition (English)

Text prepared by an organiser or implementer for the media; when several reports use near-identical wording, they often reproduce one press release.

This is a platform editorial note with no definitional source. When judging "verified", the platform treats reports with near-identical wording as one source, not independent ones, e.g. the three reports for K32.

This is a platform rule, not a judgement that the reports are unreliable: a press release may be accurate, but it cannot corroborate itself. Compare the advertorial entry: a press release is supplied text, an advertorial is paid content.
