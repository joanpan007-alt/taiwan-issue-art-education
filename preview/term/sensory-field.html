感性田野 Field of the sensible (ganxing tianye)

定義：藝術家陳界仁提出的說法，把「不可能」視為想像、創造與行動實踐的起點，用以思考身處社會內部的藝術家如何不同於社會中的其他人。

說明

呂佩怡在討論「無人可以外於社會」、藝術位於社會內部的脈絡時，引藝術家陳界仁提到的「感性田野」：「不可能恰恰就是想像、創造的開始，也是行動與實踐的開始」。

這個說法回應一個問題：若無人能置身社會之外，那麼身處社會之內的藝術家，又如何異於社會中的其他人？「感性田野」把看似的「不可能」翻轉為想像與行動的起點，呼應社會參與藝術強調過程、關係與介入現實的傾向。

本條目依呂佩怡該研究案整理；陳界仁原始說法的出處本平台未直接讀到，轉述以呂文為準。

Definition (English)

Definition: A phrase by the artist Chen Chieh-jen that takes 'the impossible' as the starting point of imagination, creation and action, used to ask how an artist within society differs from everyone else in it.

Discussing 'no one can be outside society' and art's place within society, Lu Pei-yi cites the 'field of the sensible' mentioned by the artist Chen Chieh-jen: 'the impossible is precisely the beginning of imagination and creation, and the beginning of action and practice'.

The phrase answers a question: if no one can stand outside society, how does an artist within it differ from everyone else? The 'field of the sensible' flips the seemingly 'impossible' into a starting point for imagination and action, echoing socially engaged art's stress on process, relationship and intervening in reality.

This entry follows Lu Pei-yi's research project; the source of Chen Chieh-jen's original wording was not read directly, and it is relayed as given there.
