名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

藝術節與藝術祭 Arts festivals

定義：在特定期間、多以某個地方為舞台，集中展演、工作坊與活動的藝術活動。

說明

一項台灣研究指出，各地藝術祭持續讓參與者重新感受地方的人文與環境，並重新建構地方文化的歷史脈絡。另一篇論文把藝術節與文化交流、藝術教育連結在一起討論。

平台把藝術節和駐村放在同一類，因為案例中兩者常一起出現，例如浪漫台三線藝術季、成龍濕地環境藝術節。

Definition (English)

Definition: An arts event that concentrates performances, workshops and activities within a set period, often staged in a particular place.

One Taiwanese study notes that local art festivals keep letting participants rediscover the people and environment of a place and rebuild its cultural history. Another thesis discusses arts festivals together with cultural exchange and arts education.

The platform groups festivals with residencies because the two often appear together in cases, for example the Romantic Route 3 Art Festival and the Chenglong Wetlands Environmental Art Festival.
