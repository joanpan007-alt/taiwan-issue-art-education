聲音（聲音藝術） Sound art

把聲音同時當作媒材和主題的藝術。

說明

Tate 的藝術名詞表把聲音藝術定義為「Art which uses sound both as its medium (what it is made out of) and as its subject (what it is about)」，也就是聲音既是創作的材料，也是作品談的內容。Tate 並追溯到 20 世紀初未來主義者 Luigi Russolo 的「noise machines」，以及 John Cage 1952 年的《4'33''》。

平台的案例裡，聲音多半和田野採集、口述訪談或地景結合，例如《眾聲象》以聲音採集和水管聲音裝置呈現成功鎮的海岸與港口。「聲音」在這裡是形式標籤，不是每個案例都符合 Tate 所說的「聲音藝術」。

Definition (English)

Art that uses sound as both its medium and its subject.

Tate's art terms define sound art as "Art which uses sound both as its medium (what it is made out of) and as its subject (what it is about)." Tate traces it back to Luigi Russolo's "noise machines" in the early twentieth century and John Cage's 4'33'' in 1952.

In the platform's cases, sound is mostly combined with fieldwork, oral interviews or landscape, for example Voices and Elephants, which turns sound recording and a pipe-based sound installation into a portrait of Chenggong's coast and harbour. Here "sound" is a form label; not every case fits what Tate calls sound art.
