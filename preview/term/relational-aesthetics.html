名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

關係美學 Relational Aesthetics

定義：法國藝評家布里歐於 1998 年提出的概念，把「與觀眾建立關係」的作品視為一種特殊藝術類別，讓非藝術的社會行為成為創作形式。

說明

依呂佩怡的研究，法國藝評家尼可拉·布里歐（Nicolas Bourriaud）於九〇年代中透過展覽及論述，並在 1998 年出版《關係美學》（Relational Aesthetics），掀起這股實踐風潮。這類與觀眾建立關係的作品被視為一種特殊藝術類別，其中主導的氣氛是「共同饗宴」「大家一起來」，讓非藝術的社會行為變成創作形式，而「人」的參與使社會行為閃現意義。

呂文把關係美學放在社會參與藝術的歐陸脈絡中，與英國藝評與學者 Claire Bishop 回溯的歷史並置——Bishop 把這類對「藝術與社會」的想像與實驗上溯至 1920 年代現代主義（未來派、達達），以及六〇年代末、七〇年代的偶發藝術（Happening）、福魯克薩斯（Fluxus）與情境主義國際。

本條目依呂佩怡該研究案整理；布里歐與 Bishop 的原著本平台未直接讀到，概念轉述以該文為準。

Pablo Helguera 在《社會參與藝術的十個關鍵概念》中，也把「關係美學」視為後來「社會實踐」一詞的前身——相對於去掉「藝術」二字的「社會實踐」，「關係美學」在源起時仍保留了「美學」二字。

Definition (English)

Definition: A concept proposed by the French critic Nicolas Bourriaud in 1998, treating works that 'build a relationship with the audience' as a distinct category of art and turning non-art social behaviour into a form of creation.

Per Lu Pei-yi's study, the French critic Nicolas Bourriaud, through exhibitions and writing in the mid-1990s and his 1998 book Relational Aesthetics, set off this wave of practice. Such works, which build a relationship with the audience, are treated as a distinct category of art; the governing mood is a 'shared feast', 'everyone join in', turning non-art social behaviour into a form of creation, with the participation of 'people' making social behaviour flicker with meaning.

Lu places relational aesthetics in the European lineage of socially engaged art, alongside the history traced by the British critic and scholar Claire Bishop, who takes this imagining and experiment on 'art and society' back to 1920s modernism (Futurism, Dada) and to the Happenings, Fluxus and Situationist International of the late 1960s and 1970s.

This entry follows Lu Pei-yi's research project; the platform has not read Bourriaud's or Bishop's originals directly, and relays the concepts as given there.

Pablo Helguera, in Education for Socially Engaged Art, also treats 'relational aesthetics' as a forerunner of the later term 'social practice': unlike 'social practice', which drops the word 'art', 'relational aesthetics' kept the word 'aesthetics' at its origin.
