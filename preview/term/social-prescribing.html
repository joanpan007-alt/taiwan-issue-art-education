名詞解釋 ／ 議題與社會背景

社會處方（社會處方箋） Social prescribing

由醫療人員把人轉介到藝文、園藝、運動、共餐等社區資源，以促進身心健康。

說明

社會處方「旨在透過社區活動、生活支持及資源連結，有效提供個人化照護的實際需求，進而促進公眾健康與福祉」；此概念源自英國、已推行逾 30 年，在台灣全民健保制度下，醫師並未被授權開立「社會處方（箋）」。

中正大學 USR 研討會的報導（1111 新聞，2025 年）把這個理念描述為「強調以非藥物性、社會性支持資源促進身心健康」，轉介的資源包括藝文活動、園藝、運動、志工服務或共餐據點，並說這個理念起源於英國。

平台的案例中，廣慈社宅（K13）的工作坊名單裡有「社會處方箋」。報導沒有說明它是否就是這裡所說的概念，所以不能直接等同；以上兩篇來源也都是報導，不是官方定義。

Definition (English)

Health workers refer people to community resources such as arts, gardening, exercise and shared meals to improve physical and mental health.

A 2025 Business Today report says social prescribing "aims, through community activities, life support and resource linking, to meet the real needs of personalised care and thereby promote public health and wellbeing," and describes Britain's more than 30 years of practice; it also notes that under Taiwan's National Health Insurance, doctors are not authorised to write social prescriptions.

A report on a Chung Cheng University USR seminar (1111 News, 2025) describes the idea as "emphasising non-drug, social support resources to promote physical and mental health," with referrals to resources such as arts activities, gardening, exercise, volunteering or shared-meal sites, and says it originated in Britain.

Among the platform's cases, the workshop list of the Guangci social housing project (K13) includes "social prescription notes." The report does not say whether it is the concept described here, so they cannot simply be equated; both sources above are also news reports rather than official definitions.
