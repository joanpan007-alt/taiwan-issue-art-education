名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

藝術/社會（由「藝術←→社會」轉向） Art/Society (the turn from 'art ↔ society')

定義：呂佩怡提出的概念轉變——藝術與社會的關係，從把兩者視為各自獨立、彼此往來的「藝術←→社會」，轉向視藝術為社會內部一環的「藝術/社會」。

說明

呂佩怡認為，2000 年之後台灣社會參與藝術在概念上對「藝術與社會」的想像擴大，由「藝術←→社會」轉向「藝術/社會」。前者把藝術與社會視為兩個各自獨立、再彼此往來的範疇；後者則主張藝術不在社會的外部，而在社會的內部。

她引黃孫權在《典藏今藝術》專欄的回應為佐證：「我們在乎社會或如何在乎社會是個假問題，無人可以外於社會」，認為把社會與藝術預先劃分，不過是一種掩飾無力的修辭。呂文並以台灣用語的變化勾勒這條軸線：九〇年代初談「藝術下鄉」、九〇年代末以「介入」為概念、2000 年之後強調「參與」，到 2006 年北回歸線環境藝術行動提出如何與偏鄉社區居民產生關連的提問。

本條目依呂佩怡該研究案整理；黃孫權專欄原文本平台未直接讀到，轉述以該文為準。

Definition (English)

Definition: A conceptual shift Lu Pei-yi proposes — the relation of art and society moves from 'art ↔ society', which treats the two as separate and in exchange, to 'art/society', which sees art as part of the inside of society.

Lu Pei-yi holds that after 2000 Taiwan's socially engaged art enlarged its imagining of 'art and society', turning from 'art ↔ society' to 'art/society'. The former treats art and society as two separate domains that then exchange; the latter holds that art is not outside society but within it.

She cites Huang Sun-quan's reply in his Artco column: 'whether we care about society, or how, is a false question — no one can be outside society', arguing that to presuppose a divide between society and art is merely rhetoric masking powerlessness. Lu also sketches the axis through Taiwan's changing vocabulary: 'art goes to the countryside' in the early 1990s, 'intervention' in the late 1990s, 'participation' after 2000, up to the 2006 Tropic of Cancer Environmental Art Action asking how to connect with residents of remote communities.
