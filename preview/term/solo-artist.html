名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

藝術家個人創作 Individual artist's work

定義：由藝術家獨立構思與完成、不以居民共同創作為前提的作品（平台用以與參與式實踐對照的分類）。

說明

這是平台自己的工作分類，不是學術或官方定義。平台把以藝術家個人創作為主、居民或社區沒有共同創作的案例標為「藝術家個人創作」，例如林純用個展《黑湧》與侯淑姿的《高雄眷村三部曲》。

這個標籤不代表作品與社會議題無關：兩個例子都處理環境或地方記憶，只是居民的角色是被描寫或被訪談的對象，不是共同創作者。平台收錄這類案例，是為了讓讀者能與參與式案例對照；標籤依案例來源的描述判斷，來源沒寫到居民參與，不等於實際上沒有。

Definition (English)

Definition: Work conceived and made by an artist alone, without resident co-creation (the platform's category, used as a contrast to participatory practice).

This is the platform's own classification, not an academic or official definition. Cases whose main source describes the artist as the creator, with no resident co-creation, are tagged this way, e.g. Lin Chun-yung's solo show Black Surge and Hou Shu-tzu's Kaohsiung Military Dependents' Village Trilogy.

The tag does not mean the work is unrelated to social issues: both examples deal with the environment or local memory, but residents are subjects or interviewees, not co-creators. The tag follows what the case sources say; a source silent on participation does not prove there was none.
