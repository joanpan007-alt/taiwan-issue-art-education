白色恐怖／轉型正義 White Terror / transitional justice

「威權統治時期」指 1945/8/15 至 1992/11/6；轉型正義是民主化後，對過去國家大規模侵害人權所做的平復工作。「白色恐怖」是這段時期政治迫害的俗稱。

說明

依行政院推動轉型正義會報的問答集（引《促進轉型正義條例》第 3 條第 1 款），『威權統治時期』自民國 34 年（1945）8 月 15 日起，至 81 年（1992）11 月 6 日、即金門馬祖東沙南沙地區宣告解嚴前一日止。『轉型正義』依行政院人權資訊網（引聯合國）指：國家結束威權統治、邁向民主與和平時，承認並平復過去國家大規模侵害人權的不正義；晚近聯合國報告提出真相、正義、賠償、保證不再發生與追憶五個支柱。

政府依《促進轉型正義條例》分工推動多項任務：開放政治檔案（國家發展委員會）、處置威權象徵（內政部）、保存不義遺址（文化部）、平復司法不法與行政不法（法務部）、療癒政治受難者與家屬的創傷（衛生福利部）、推動轉型正義教育（教育部）、調查及處理不當黨產（不當黨產處理委員會）。『不義遺址』依該條例第 5 條第 2 項，指『威權統治時期，統治者大規模侵害人權事件之發生地』。

『白色恐怖』是這段威權時期政治迫害（逮捕、審判、關押與處決政治犯及異議者）的俗稱，為平台整理用語、非法律名詞。綠島是代表性的不義遺址之一，設有白色恐怖綠島紀念園區（由文化部所屬國家人權博物館經營）。平台案例中，記憶‧邊緣——綠島人權藝術季北藝大特展（K64）由北藝大與國家人權博物館合作，師生先透過『記憶政治與藝術介入』課程認識白色恐怖與轉型正義脈絡，再進駐綠島紀念園區現地創作。

要提醒的是：本詞條的『威權統治時期』起訖與各部會分工，依行政院兩個官方頁面（轉型正義問答集、人權資訊網）整理，《促進轉型正義條例》逐條原文本次未另行讀取，請以全國法規資料庫現行條文為準。

Definition (English)

The authoritarian period runs from 15 Aug 1945 to 6 Nov 1992; transitional justice is the post-democratization work of redressing the state’s past large-scale human-rights violations. White Terror is the common name for the political persecution of that era.

Per the Executive Yuan Transitional Justice Board’s Q&A (citing Article 3(1) of the Act on Promoting Transitional Justice), the authoritarian period runs from 15 Aug 1945 to 6 Nov 1992 — the day before martial law was lifted in Kinmen, Matsu and the Dongsha and Nansha areas. Transitional justice, per the Executive Yuan’s human-rights site (citing the UN), is a state’s acknowledgement and redress, as it ends authoritarian rule and moves toward democracy and peace, of past large-scale human-rights injustices; recent UN reports set out five pillars: truth, justice, reparation, guarantees of non-recurrence, and memorialization.

Under the Act the government divides the work among agencies: opening political archives (National Development Council), dealing with authoritarian symbols (Ministry of the Interior), preserving sites of injustice (Ministry of Culture), redressing judicial and administrative wrongs (Ministry of Justice), healing the trauma of victims and their families (Ministry of Health and Welfare), transitional-justice education (Ministry of Education), and investigating and handling ill-gotten party assets (Ill-gotten Party Assets Settlement Committee). A site of injustice, per Article 5(2), is a place where the rulers committed large-scale human-rights violations during the authoritarian period.

White Terror is the common name for the political persecution of that authoritarian era — the arrest, trial, imprisonment and execution of political prisoners and dissidents; it is the platform’s shorthand, not a legal term. Green Island is one of the best-known sites of injustice, home to the White Terror Green Island Memorial Park (run by the National Human Rights Museum under the Ministry of Culture). Among the platform’s cases, Memory on the Margins — the TNUA special exhibition at the 2020 Green Island Human Rights Art Festival (K64) is a collaboration between TNUA and the National Human Rights Museum, in which students first learn the White Terror and transitional-justice context through a memory-politics-and-artistic-intervention course, then create on-site at the Green Island memorial park.

A caveat: the dates of the authoritarian period and the agency responsibilities here are drawn from two Executive Yuan official pages; the full article-by-article text of the Act on Promoting Transitional Justice was not separately read this time, so please refer to the current text in the national Laws & Regulations Database.
