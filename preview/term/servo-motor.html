名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

伺服機動（Servo Motor） Servo Motor

定義：藝評家陳泰松用來比喻藝術家社會角色的詞——把藝術家視為對社會體制進行干預的「裝置」的操作者，藉以創造新的社會關係。

說明

依呂佩怡的研究，陳泰松在 2010 年一場台新座談會中，將藝術家視為「社會的交往者」，追問「藝術家如何透過創作實踐與社會交往？」，並把藝術家視為「伺服機動」（Servo Motor）的操作者。

「伺服機動」原意為「伺服馬達」或「伺動馬達」，被引申為一種對社會體制運作進行干預的裝置，而藝術家就是操作這種裝置的人，可以藉此創造新的社會關係。這個比喻與呂文所談的「中介」相呼應，都把藝術家放在社會內部、作為促成改變的操作者。

本條目依呂佩怡該研究案整理；陳泰松說法原出〈藝術家：社會的交往者〉專題（《典藏今藝術》，2010），本平台未直接讀到原文，轉述以呂文為準。

相關名詞

作者論・中介・去作者（藝術家角色）藝術/社會（由「藝術←→社會」轉向）

Definition (English)

Definition: A metaphor the critic Chen Tai-song uses for the artist's social role — the artist as operator of a 'device' that intervenes in the workings of the social system, so as to create new social relations.

Per Lu Pei-yi's study, at a 2010 Taishin forum Chen Tai-song cast the artist as 'society's go-between', asking 'how does the artist engage society through creative practice?', and saw the artist as the operator of a 'Servo Motor'.

'Servo Motor' originally names a servomotor; it is extended here to a device that intervenes in the workings of the social system, with the artist as the one who operates it and can thereby create new social relations. The metaphor echoes Lu's notion of the 'mediator', both placing the artist inside society as an operator who brings about change.

This entry follows Lu Pei-yi's research project; Chen Tai-song's formulation comes from the feature 'The Artist: Society's Go-Between' (Artco, 2010), which the platform has not read directly, relayed here as given by Lu.
