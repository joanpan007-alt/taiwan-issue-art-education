閩東語（馬祖話） Eastern Min (Matsu dialect)

通行於馬祖的在地語言，屬閩東語，已列入十二年國教本土語文課程。

說明

國家教育研究院的《本土語文（閩東語文）課程手冊》顯示，閩東語文已納入十二年國民基本教育課程綱要的本土語文領域，自 111 學年度（2022）起實施，成為正式的本土語文課程之一。這反映閩東語（馬祖話）作為地方語言的保存與傳承，已進入制度化的語言復振。

馬祖國際藝術島（K46）的核心議題之一，就是人口流失下的閩東文化與語言復振；報導寫到計畫與馬祖青年發展協會等在地單位合作。這個名詞的定義由平台整理，課綱資訊依國教院手冊。

The local language of Matsu, a form of Eastern Min; now part of the national mother-tongue curriculum.

The National Academy for Educational Research's course handbook for Eastern Min shows it has been incorporated into the mother-tongue strand of the 12-year basic-education curriculum, in effect from the 2022 school year. This reflects an institutionalised language revival for Eastern Min (the Matsu dialect).

One of the core issues of Matsu International Art Island (K46) is Eastern Min culture and language revival amid depopulation; reports note the project worked with local groups such as the Matsu Youth Development Association.
