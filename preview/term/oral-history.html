名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

口述歷史 Oral history

請人講述親身經歷並記錄保存，讓一般人的記憶成為歷史資料。

說明

口述歷史是一種歷史與社會科學的研究方法，通常由訪談者和敘述者坐下來交談，記錄並保存對方的生命故事，或某個事件、某段人生的記憶。它以人為中心，讓原住民、婦女、移民、勞動者等較少被書寫的群體也能講述自己的過去。

許多案例以居民口述為素材，例如侯淑姿以劉奶奶的口述為主軸的高雄眷村三部曲，以及廣慈社宅取材自居民口述的夢想。

Definition (English)

Asking people to tell their own experiences and recording them, so ordinary people's memories become historical material.

Oral history is a research method in history and the social sciences. An interviewer and a narrator usually sit down to talk, and the narrator's life story or memory of an event is recorded and preserved. It is people-centered, letting groups less often written about, such as Indigenous peoples, women, migrants and workers, tell their own pasts.

Many cases use residents' spoken accounts as material, for example Hou Shu-tzu's Kaohsiung Military Dependents' Village Trilogy, built around Grandma Liu's account, and the Guangci social housing works drawn from residents' dreams told in interviews.
