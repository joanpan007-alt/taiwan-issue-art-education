名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

生態博物館 Ecomuseum

在特定群落或地區的社會、文化與自然環境脈絡中，呈現當地歷史與傳統的跨領域博物館。

說明

中央研究院 AAT-Taiwan 藝術與建築索引典對這個詞的說明是：「在某特定群落或地區之社會、文化，和自然環境的背景脈絡中，呈現其歷史與傳統的跨領域博物館。」詞條的來源包括國家教育研究院樂詞網等。

平台的案例中，義家藝館的計畫構想把榮民之家轉化為「概念性生態博物館」；GPI 書評寫到計畫每年在國際博物館日將臺北榮譽國民之家轉化為這樣的空間。案例的來源沒有解釋這個詞，上面的定義是平台另外查到的，義家藝館是否完全符合這個定義，請讀者自行判斷。

Definition (English)

An interdisciplinary museum that presents the history and heritage of a particular community or region in the context of its society, culture and natural environment.

The Academia Sinica AAT-Taiwan thesaurus describes the term as "interdisciplinary museums that present the history and heritage of a particular community or region in the context of its society, culture, and natural environment." Its sources include Taiwan's National Academy for Educational Research terminology bank.

Among the platform's cases, the Yijia Art Museum project envisions turning the veterans' home into a "conceptual ecomuseum", and the GPI review says the project turns the Taipei Honor Home for Veterans into such a space on International Museum Day each year. The cases' sources do not explain the term; the definition above was found separately by the platform, and readers should judge whether Yijia fully fits it.
