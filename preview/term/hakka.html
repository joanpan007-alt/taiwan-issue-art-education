客庄／客家 Hakka villages / Hakka (kezhuang)

客家基本法定義客家人：具客家血緣或淵源且自我認同；「客庄」是常用語，法條沒有定義。

說明

《客家基本法》第 2 條定義「客家人」為「具有客家血緣或客家淵源，且自我認同為客家人者」。這一條還定義了客家族群、客語、客家人口、客家事務；「客家文化重點發展區」出現在第 4、8、9、12 條，條文沒有在第 2 條另行定義（本詞條讀的是臺北市法規查詢系統轉載的版本，標示最近修正日期為民國 107 年 1 月 31 日，請以現行條文為準）。

「客庄」是一般用語，平台沒有找到法定定義，在案例中通常指客家人聚居的聚落，例如浪漫台三線與《藝耆共舞．客庄日常》；是否把某地稱作客庄，以案例來源的用語為準。

Definition (English)

The Hakka Basic Act defines a Hakka person as one with Hakka ancestry or origin who identifies as Hakka; "kezhuang" (Hakka village) is common usage the law does not define.

Article 2 of the Hakka Basic Act defines a Hakka person as one "with Hakka blood or Hakka origin who self-identifies as Hakka". It also defines Hakka ethnic group, Hakka language, population and affairs; "Hakka cultural priority development areas" appear in Articles 4, 8, 9 and 12 but are not defined in Article 2. "Kezhuang" is everyday usage with no legal definition found; in cases it usually means settlements where Hakka people live, e.g. Romantic Route 3 and Elder Dance, Hakka Daily Life. Whether a place is called kezhuang follows the case source.
