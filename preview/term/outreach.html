名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

藝術推廣 Arts outreach

把藝術帶給更多一般民眾，增加接觸與欣賞的機會。在台灣的法規與補助中常見，但查到的法規都沒有單獨給出定義。

說明

「推廣」在台灣的法規裡是常見用語。文化藝術獎助及促進條例把文化藝術的「創作、展演及推廣」並列為事務範圍，並規定主管機關得獎勵或補助「創作、發展及推廣」；條文只使用這個詞，沒有另行定義（民國 110 年修正版本）。教育部的各級學校藝術推廣教育實施辦法第 2 條則說明學校得「為普及藝術欣賞人口，提升藝術素養，得對社會大眾辦理各類藝術推廣教育」，這是查到最接近目的說明的條文，但同樣不是定義。

政策上的用法大多指「把作品或團隊送到觀眾面前」。例如 2024 年文化部的「藝術入校」補助，讓藝文團體申請進入學校，公視報導用「推廣」描述這件事，也提到團體希望補助能持續，推廣與傳承才有長久的效果；報導沒有給出定義。

平台提醒：沒有來源說明「藝術推廣」與「藝術教育」或「藝術下鄉」有何不同，三者在實務上常重疊（例如台灣芭蕾舞團的計畫名稱就同時含有「藝術下鄉」與「藝術推廣」）。以下只是平台的工作區分，不是來源的說法：推廣偏重讓更多人接觸作品，參與式藝術偏重居民參與創作的過程。這個區分是否適用，請讀者自行判斷。

Definition (English)

Bringing art to more of the general public and widening chances to encounter it. Common in Taiwan's laws and funding, but none of the laws found defines it separately.

"Promotion" (tuiguang) is a common word in Taiwan's laws. The Act for the Development of Cultural and Artistic Activities lists the "creation, presentation and promotion" of culture and the arts as part of its scope and lets the competent authorities reward or subsidise "creation, development and promotion"; the text uses the word without defining it (version amended in 2021). The Ministry of Education's Regulations on Arts Promotion Education in Schools, Article 2, says schools may run arts promotion education for the public "to broaden the audience for art and raise artistic literacy"; this is the closest statement of purpose found, but it is not a definition either.

In policy the word mostly means getting works or companies in front of audiences. For example, the Ministry of Culture's 2024 "Art into Schools" subsidy lets arts groups apply to enter schools; PTS News describes this as promotion and notes that groups want the funding to continue so promotion and transmission last. The report gives no definition.

Platform note: no source explains how arts outreach differs from arts education or "art to the countryside," and in practice they overlap (Taiwan Ballet's project title, for instance, contains both 'art to the countryside' and 'arts promotion'). The following is only the platform's working distinction, not a claim from the sources: outreach stresses letting more people encounter works, while participatory art stresses residents taking part in making them. Readers should judge whether it fits.
