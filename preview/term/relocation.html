遷村 Village relocation

因開發或政策，整個聚落的居民集體搬遷到別處。

說明

以高雄大林蒲為例，遷村安置計畫由經濟部與高雄市政府協商，並經行政院核定。2026 年 4 月起辦理協議價購說明會，截至 7 月底超過 2,500 名所有權人簽署同意書；安置地的公共工程已先行，預計 2027 年啟動抽籤配地與補償費發價。

遷村的一般定義是編輯整理。大林蒲的案例都和這段過程有關，包括大鼻青年的絹印工作坊、玫好時光 Podcast。該報導沒有說明遷村原因。

學理來源

  1. 〈高雄市府推大林蒲遷村進度 安置地道路已開工〉，中央社（2026-08-05）

Definition (English)

Residents of a whole settlement move together to another place because of development or policy.

Take Dalinpu in Kaohsiung. According to CNA in August 2026, the relocation and resettlement plan was negotiated by the Ministry of Economic Affairs and the Kaohsiung city government and approved by the Executive Yuan. Briefings on negotiated land purchase began in April 2026; by the end of July more than 2,500 owners had signed consent forms; public works at the resettlement site began first, with lottery allocation and compensation payments expected to start in 2027.

The general definition of relocation here is an editorial summary. The Dalinpu cases all touch this process, including Big Nose Youth's silkscreen workshop and the Good Times podcast. The report does not state why the relocation is happening.
