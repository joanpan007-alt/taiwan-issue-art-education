大地藝術祭 Land art festival

把作品設置在鄉野與地方生活裡，並與居民長期合作的藝術祭。

說明

最常被提起的是日本的越後妻有大地藝術祭，由藝術總監北川富朗於 2000 年起策劃，每三年舉辦一次。作品多為現地製作，也有藝術家利用廢校、空屋等閒置空間創作。北川希望藝術為被遺忘的村落注入活力，因此長年與居民溝通互動。

注意，「大地藝術祭」與英文 land art（大地藝術）不完全相同，這裡指的是前者。目前案例庫中有一例直接使用此詞：2026 年台南官田的「Mattauw 大地藝術季」（K32），目前標為待查證。

Definition (English)

An art festival that sets works in the countryside and local life and works with residents over the long term.

The best-known example is Japan's Echigo-Tsumari Art Triennale, planned by artistic director Fram Kitagawa since 2000 and held every three years. Many works are made on site, and some artists use disused schools and empty houses. Kitagawa hoped art could bring energy to forgotten villages, so he has long communicated with residents.

Note that "land art festival" is not quite the same as the English term land art; this entry means the former. One case in the directory uses this term directly: the 2026 Mattauw Land Art Season in Guantian, Tainan (K32), currently marked as pending verification.
