名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

作者論・中介・去作者（藝術家角色） Author / mediator / de-author (roles of the artist)

定義：呂佩怡用來描述社會參與藝術中藝術家位置的三種角色——作為主體的「作者論」、作為轉化介面的「中介」，以及退居幕後的「去作者」。

說明

呂佩怡在〈轉向「藝術/社會」〉中指出，2000 年之後台灣社會參與藝術的藝術家角色可分為三類：藝術家作為主體（作者論）、藝術家作為轉化介面（中介）、以及藝術家退後（去作者）；這組區分對應她提出的另一條主張——由「藝術←→社會」轉向「藝術/社會」。

「作者論」指藝術家以其敏銳之眼，與整體社會之間產生一種有距離的批判。「中介」指藝術家以創意在系統機制之內轉換、使自身成為一個交換平台，以雙贏策略促成不同領域或立場雙方的合作，並且必須強調其藝術家身分、依賴並挪用藝術機制；呂文以周育正、黃博志為例（如周育正《東亞照明》《虹牌油漆》(2011) 中要求廠商贊助、再轉化為美術館資源的「回饋」機制）。策展人鄭慧華也形容「藝術創作其實更像一種開創性的『中介』，而藝術家則像一個『中介者』（mediator）」。「去作者」則指藝術家在創作中退居幕後。

本條目依呂佩怡該研究案整理；文中所引藝術家（周育正、黃博志等）尚未個別收錄為本平台案例，另於案例資料庫逐一查證後補入。

Definition (English)

Definition: Lu Pei-yi's three roles for the artist's position in socially engaged art — the artist as subject ('authorship'), as a transforming interface ('mediator'), and stepping back ('de-author').

In 'Turning to Art/Society', Lu Pei-yi argues that after 2000 the artist's role in Taiwan's socially engaged art can be divided into three: the artist as subject (authorship), the artist as a transforming interface (mediator), and the artist stepping back (de-author). This division matches her other claim — the turn from 'art ↔ society' to 'art/society'.

'Authorship' means the artist, with a sharp eye, produces a distanced critique of society at large. 'Mediator' means the artist ingeniously converts things from within the system's mechanisms, becoming an exchange platform and using win-win strategies to broker cooperation between parties of different fields or positions, while still asserting an artist identity and relying on and appropriating the art apparatus; Lu cites Chou Yu-cheng and Huang Po-chih (e.g. Chou's TOA Lighting and Hong's Paint, 2011, which turn sponsors' donated lamps and paint into museum resources as a 'feedback' mechanism). The curator Amy Cheng likewise calls artistic creation 'a kind of pioneering mediation, with the artist as a mediator'. 'De-author' means the artist recedes into the background.

This entry follows Lu Pei-yi's research project; the artists cited (Chou Yu-cheng, Huang Po-chih and others) are not yet individually included as platform cases, and will be added after case-by-case verification.
