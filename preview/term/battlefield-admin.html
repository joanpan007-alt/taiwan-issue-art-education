戰地政務 Battlefield (military) administration

冷戰時期在金門、馬祖實施的軍事管制體制，由軍方統管行政，1992 年解除。

說明

金門自 1956 年進入戰地政務體制，金門與馬祖的戰地政務於 1992 年 11 月 7 日解除；在管制時期，一般人民很難自由往來金馬，除官方、軍方與受邀者外，多半只有海外華僑有機會登島，1994 年《金馬安輔條例》修正後才廢除出入境管制。

戰地政務時期軍方統管地方行政，居民生活受到相當限制。這個名詞的概括定義由平台整理，沒有引用法規原文。馬祖國際藝術島（K46）正是在戰地政務解除後，把一度封閉的據點、碉堡與防空洞重新轉化為創作與展演空間，處理軍管時期被隱藏的常民記憶。

Definition (English)

The military-rule system imposed on Kinmen and Matsu during the Cold War, under which the military ran civil administration; lifted in 1992.

According to The News Lens, Kinmen entered the battlefield-administration system in 1956, and military rule over Kinmen and Matsu was lifted on 7 November 1992; during the controlled period ordinary people could hardly travel freely to the islands, and entry controls were abolished only after the 1994 amendment of the relevant act.

Under battlefield administration the military ran local civil affairs and residents' lives were considerably restricted.
