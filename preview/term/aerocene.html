大氣世（Aerocene） Aerocene

藝術家 Tomás Saraceno 發起的計畫，以不依賴氦氣、電池或太陽能板的漂浮雕塑為核心。

說明

MIT Arts 的專文說明，Aerocene 的作品是「emission-free floating sculptures」，以銀色與透明 Mylar 製成，設計成不靠氦氣、電池或太陽能板環繞地球，借風滑行，並由太陽與紅外線輻射維持浮力。發起人是藝術家 Tomás Saraceno，合作者包括 MIT 的氣象學家與研究員；2015 年 8 月團隊在索羅門群島測試，12 月作品在巴黎 COP21 氣候峰會期間展出。

在台灣，共織宇宙（K17）是這個計畫的本地版本。K17 的來源指出，作品屬藝術家「大氣世」計畫，台灣版本包含台灣民眾的作品。MIT 的專文沒有提到計畫是否開源，所以平台不寫這一點。

Definition (English)

A project started by artist Tomás Saraceno, centred on floating sculptures that do not rely on helium, batteries or solar panels.

An MIT Arts feature describes Aerocene's works as "emission-free floating sculptures," made of silver and transparent Mylar and designed to travel around the earth without helium, batteries or solar panels, gliding on wind currents and kept afloat by solar and infrared radiation. The initiator is artist Tomás Saraceno, with collaborators including an MIT meteorologist and a researcher; in August 2015 the team tested in the Solomon Islands, and in December the work was shown during the COP21 climate summit in Paris.

In Taiwan, Co-Weaving Universe (K17) is the local version of this project. K17's sources say the work belongs to the artist's Aerocene project and that the Taiwan version includes works by Taiwanese participants. The MIT feature does not say whether the project is open source, so the platform does not state it.
