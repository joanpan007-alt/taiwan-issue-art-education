社會住宅（社宅） Social housing

政府興辦或獎勵民間興辦、專供出租的住宅。

說明

依住宅法第 3 條第 2 款，社會住宅指「由政府興辦或獎勵民間興辦，專供出租之用之住宅及其必要附屬設施」（民國 115 年 3 月 4 日修正版本）。

社宅常是議題本位藝術的場域，因為住戶來自不同背景，需要建立彼此的關係。平台裡的廣慈社宅、未來學家（台中社宅）都在社宅進行。

學理來源

  1. 住宅法第 3 條（民國 115 年 3 月 4 日修正版本）

Definition (English)

Housing built by government or with government incentives, for rent only.

Under Article 3, item 2 of the Housing Act, social housing means "housing and necessary ancillary facilities built by the government or by private parties with incentives, solely for rent" (version amended March 4, 2026).

Social housing is often a site for issue-based art because residents come from different backgrounds and need to build relationships. Guangci Social Housing and The Futurists (Taichung social housing) in the directory both took place in social housing.
