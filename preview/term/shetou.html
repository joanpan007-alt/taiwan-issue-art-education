社頭 Shetou (procession guilds)

大溪遶境慶典中，居民依同行業或地緣自發組成的表演團體，各有獨特藝陣。

說明

『社頭』是地方自發組成的遶境團體，常因同行業或地緣關係而形成，各社的藝陣因此有獨特風格，例子包括與職業相關的牛犁陣、墨斗陣、土水仙童；慶典中也有頭旗、龍陣、獅陣、童仔、北管、神將等表演。

在平台案例中，大溪大禧（K48）以普濟堂關聖帝君聖誕遶境為核心，社頭、廟方與居民是慶典的主體，2026 年版有 32 個社頭參與。社頭是理解這個案例『居民參與』的關鍵——參與的單位不是個人，而是既有的在地團體。

相關案例（1）

大溪大禧（系列）

Definition (English)

In Daxi's deity-procession festival, performance troupes that residents form by trade or locality, each with its own art-array style.

The Daxi Daxi official site explains that a 'shetou' is a self-organised procession troupe, often formed by shared trade or locality, so each troupe's art-array has its own style; examples tied to occupations include the ox-plough, ink-line and plasterer-boy troupes, and the festival also features lead banners, dragon and lion dances, child figures, beiguan music and the shen-jiang generals.

Among the platform's cases, Daxi Daxi (K48) centres on the birthday procession of Lord Guan at Puji Temple, with shetou, temple staff and residents as its core; the 2026 edition had 32 shetou. The shetou are key to reading this case's 'resident participation' — the unit of participation is not the individual but an existing local group.
