培力（賦權） Empowerment

讓人或社區逐步有能力自己提出問題、解決問題。

說明

一篇研究把培力（empowerment）說明為：社區透過完整的支援系統與培力過程，逐步具備自行解決或改善所處環境問題的能力。在社區層次上，它強調社區主動提案、自主處理，而不是由上而下主導。

培力是否真的達成，是不容易衡量的問題。例如未來學家那篇評論就質疑，以 KPI 衡量社群培力是否恰當。

學理來源

  1. 林書嫻，《提案型環境營造社區培力之探討》碩士論文（2012）

Definition (English)

Helping people or communities gradually gain the ability to raise and solve problems themselves.

One study explains empowerment as a community gradually gaining the ability to solve or improve its own environmental problems through a complete support system and process. At the community level, it stresses communities proposing and handling things themselves rather than being directed from above.

Whether empowerment is truly achieved is hard to measure. The Futurists review, for example, questions whether measuring community empowerment by KPIs is appropriate.
