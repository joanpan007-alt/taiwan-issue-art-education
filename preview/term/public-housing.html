平宅（平價住宅） Public housing (pingzhai)

政府為低收入戶興建的住宅，台北安康平宅是全國規模最大的一處。

說明

平宅是政府為低收入戶興建的住宅。台北的安康平宅於 1975 年興建，共 1,020 戶，是全國規模最大的平價住宅；同年也安置了一批越南難僑。

報導也寫到平宅長期被汙名化，居民有時不願對外說出住址。2012 至 2013 年間，市府確立分三期改建為「興隆公共住宅」的方案。安康平宅「移動的自由」就是在居民搬遷前辦的展覽。

學理來源

  1. 張子午，〈台北市最後的貧民窟：安康社區的過去、現在與未來〉，報導者（2018-07-23）

Definition (English)

Housing the government built for low-income households; Taipei's Ankang is the largest in Taiwan.

According to The Reporter's long feature, pingzhai is housing the government built for low-income households. Taipei's Ankang public housing was built in 1975 with 1,020 units, the largest such estate in Taiwan; in the same year it also housed a group of Vietnamese refugees.

The report also describes long-standing stigma, with residents sometimes reluctant to say where they live. In 2012–2013 the city settled on a plan to rebuild it in three phases as Xinglong Public Housing. "Freedom of Movement" was an exhibition held before residents moved out.
