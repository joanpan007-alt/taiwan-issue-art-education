原住民族文化健康站（文健站） Indigenous Cultural Health Station

原住民族委員會在部落與都會原住民聚落推動的長者照顧與文化傳承據點。

說明

行政院 2020 年的說明指出，原住民族委員會依據長期照顧十年計畫 2.0 的原住民族專章，以「族人照顧族人」、「因族因地制宜」的模式，在原住民族地區及都會原住民族聚落推動文健站。頁面稱文健站不只照顧長者，也是幼兒、青年及照服員學習族語和傳統文化的據點。

新北市原住民族行政局 2019 年的實施計畫補充：107 年起，原「原住民老人日間關懷站」更名為原住民文化健康站；服務對象是 55 歲以上健康、亞健康、衰弱的原住民長者及輕度失能長者，服務含到宅關懷、共餐、健康促進活動等。

平台的案例中，補龍計畫（K19）有一位藝術家引導文健站的長者，把埤塘生活經驗轉為版畫素材。這個詞條只說明文健站是什麼，不代表平台評價它的成效。

Definition (English)

Elder-care and cultural-transmission sites promoted by the Council of Indigenous Peoples in tribal areas and urban Indigenous settlements.

The Executive Yuan's 2020 note says the Council of Indigenous Peoples, under the Indigenous chapter of the Long-term Care 10-Year Plan 2.0, promotes cultural health stations in Indigenous areas and urban Indigenous settlements on a model of "our people caring for our people" and adapting to each tribe and place. It describes the stations as places that care for elders and also where children, young people and care workers learn their languages and traditional culture.

New Taipei City's Indigenous Peoples Bureau's 2019 implementation plan adds that from 2018 the former "Indigenous elders' day care stations" were renamed Indigenous cultural health stations; services target Indigenous elders aged 55 and over who are healthy, sub-healthy or frail, plus mildly disabled elders, and include home visits, shared meals and health promotion activities.

Among the platform's cases, Mend the Dragon (K19) has an artist guiding elders at a cultural health station to turn pond-life experience into printmaking material. This entry only explains what the stations are and does not assess their effectiveness.
