新住民 New immigrants

移居台灣定居的外籍與大陸配偶等，其子女稱為新住民二代。

說明

內政部移民署的《展新計畫》文件，服務對象含括新住民及二代子女；文件寫到新住民人數已超過 51 萬人、其子女超過 36 萬人（2016 年資料，未註明統計時間點）。該文件沒有明確寫出「新住民」的定義，從內容看範圍包括外籍與大陸配偶。

平台的案例中，新住民常與移工一起出現，像是 Jalan-Jalan 移路相伴特展、新住民和移工繪本創作課程。

Definition (English)

People who have settled in Taiwan through marriage or migration, such as foreign and mainland-Chinese spouses; their children are called the second generation.

The National Immigration Agency's Zhanxin Plan document covers new immigrants and their second-generation children, and says new immigrants number over 510,000 and their children over 360,000 (2016 data; no reference date given). It does not clearly define "new immigrants," but from its content the group includes foreign and mainland-Chinese spouses.

In the platform's cases new immigrants often appear with migrant workers, as in the Jalan-Jalan special exhibition and the picture-book course for new immigrants and migrant workers.
