名詞解釋 ／ 資料與計畫用語

國家文化藝術基金會（國藝會） National Culture and Arts Foundation (NCAF)

定義：1996 年成立的財團法人，依《文化藝術獎助及促進條例》設置，以營造展演環境、獎勵文化藝術事業、提升藝文水準為宗旨；是台灣視覺藝術補助的主要機構，也是本平台「藝術/社會」等名詞所依呂佩怡研究案的取樣範圍。

說明

依國藝會官網，財團法人國家文化藝術基金會（簡稱國藝會）於 1996 年 1 月正式成立，「以營造有利文化藝術工作之展演環境、獎勵文化藝術事業、提升藝文水準為機構服務宗旨」；業務依〈國家文化藝術基金會設置條例〉，包括輔導辦理文化藝術活動、贊助各項文化藝術事業、獎助文化藝術工作者，以及執行文化藝術獎助條例所訂之任務。

其法源可見於《文化藝術獎助及促進條例》第五章：第 24 條第 1 項明定「為輔導辦理文化藝術活動，贊助藝文事業，設置財團法人國家文化藝術基金會」，並應定期頒發各類國家文藝獎、訂定各類獎勵補助相關辦法、提供文化藝術資訊及法律諮詢；第 25 條規定基金來源包含政府編列預算、基金孳息、國內外公私機構團體或個人之捐贈及其他收入。（本平台讀取的是《文化藝術獎助及促進條例》第五章；該會官網所稱〈國家文化藝術基金會設置條例〉本次未另行讀取，故業務條文以前者為準。）

對本平台而言，國藝會的意義在於它是呂佩怡〈轉向「藝術/社會」：社會參與藝術實踐研究〉的取樣範圍——該研究以國藝會美術類受補助名單為主（常態補助的創作、展覽、調查研究、出版等），並納入視覺藝術策展專案補助計畫，再擴大參考國藝會 2007–2010 年的社區／社群研究計畫。因此平台的「藝術/社會」「作者論・中介・去作者」等名詞，多由這批受補助案例歸納而來。本條目列於「資料與計畫用語」，用以說明補助機構脈絡，不替個別案例貼標籤。

Definition (English)

Definition: A foundation established in 1996 under the Culture and Arts Reward and Promotion Act, tasked with fostering a performance and exhibition environment, rewarding arts undertakings and raising the level of the arts; it is Taiwan's main funder of visual art and the sampling frame of the Lu Pei-yi study this platform's new terms draw on.

Per the NCAF official site, the National Culture and Arts Foundation (NCAF) was formally established in January 1996, with the stated mission of 'fostering an environment favourable to arts work, rewarding arts undertakings and raising the level of the arts'; its work, under the Statute for the Establishment of the NCAF, covers guiding and running cultural and arts activities, sponsoring arts undertakings, funding arts workers, and carrying out the tasks set by the arts-reward act.

Its legal basis appears in Chapter 5 of the Culture and Arts Reward and Promotion Act: Article 24(1) provides that 'to guide and run cultural and arts activities and sponsor arts undertakings, the National Culture and Arts Foundation is established', and the Foundation is to award the National Awards for Arts, set the various reward and grant rules, and provide arts information and legal counsel; Article 25 lists the fund's sources as government budget, interest on the fund, donations from public and private bodies or individuals at home and abroad, and other income. (The platform read Chapter 5 of this Act; the 'Statute for the Establishment of the NCAF' cited by the Foundation's site was not separately read, so the duties here follow the former.)

For this platform, NCAF matters as the sampling frame of Lu Pei-yi's study 'Turning to Art/Society': it draws mainly on NCAF's fine-art grant lists (regular grants for creation, exhibition, survey research and publication), its curatorial project grants in visual art, and further on NCAF's 2007-2010 community research projects. The platform's terms 'art/society' and 'author/mediator/de-author' are largely distilled from these funded cases. This entry sits under 'Data and program terms' to explain the funding-body context, and does not tag individual cases.
