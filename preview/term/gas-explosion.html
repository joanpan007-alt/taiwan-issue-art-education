高雄氣爆（81 氣爆） Kaohsiung gas explosions

2014 年 7 月 31 日深夜，高雄因丙烯外洩發生的連環氣爆。

說明

公視新聞網 2024 年的回顧報導寫道，事故發生在 2014 年 7 月 31 日深夜，高雄市因石化原料丙烯外洩引發氣爆，造成 32 人死亡、321 人輕重傷，共 3140 多人受災。報導還提到，高雄市在 2015 年通過《高雄市既有工業管理自治條例》，2024 年管線數量從氣爆前的 89 條降到 71 條。

平台目前只讀到這一個可讀的來源。「81 氣爆」的稱法來自「記憶的漣漪」（K02）的報導，這份公視報導沒有使用這個說法。

Definition (English)

The chain of gas explosions in Kaohsiung on the night of July 31, 2014, caused by a propylene leak.

A 2024 PTS News retrospective says the incident happened on the night of July 31, 2014, when a leak of the petrochemical feedstock propylene caused explosions in Kaohsiung, killing 32 people and injuring 321, with over 3,140 people affected. It also notes that Kaohsiung passed an ordinance on managing existing industry in 2015 and that, by 2024, the number of pipelines had fallen from 89 before the explosions to 71.

The platform has only this one readable source so far. The name "81 explosions" comes from the report on Ripples of Memory (K02); the PTS report does not use it.
