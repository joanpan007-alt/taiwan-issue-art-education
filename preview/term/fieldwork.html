田野調查 Fieldwork

走進現場，用觀察、訪談等方式直接蒐集資料。

說明

田野調查是在真實的生活環境中，而不是在實驗室或教室裡，觀察並蒐集人、文化與自然環境資料的過程。

平台的深度報導構想以田野訪談為基礎，記錄計畫的形成、合作與遭遇的困難。部分案例的展前也做過田野訪調，例如 Jalan-Jalan 移路相伴特展。

Definition (English)

Going to the site and gathering data directly through observation, interviews and similar means.

Fieldwork is the process of observing and collecting data about people, cultures and natural environments in real settings, rather than in a lab or classroom.

The platform's planned in-depth reporting is based on field interviews, recording how a project formed, who collaborated and what difficulties arose. Some cases also did field research before the show, such as the Jalan-Jalan special exhibition.
