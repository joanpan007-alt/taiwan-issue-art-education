名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

參與式藝術 Participatory Art

定義：以觀眾／群眾的介入與互動本身構成作品的藝術，觀眾從旁觀者轉為藝術家的合作者。

說明

依鄭林佳在《現代美術》第 177 期的整理，『參與式藝術』（Participatory Art）指透過群眾介入及互動形成的藝術創作。藝評人 Lori Waxman 認為，當創作導入觀眾參與，觀眾（audience）便不再是被傳統話語稱呼的旁觀者，而是藝術家的合作者。文章以 1993 年威尼斯雙年展開放展（Aperto 93）中 Rirkrit Tiravanija 煮湯宴客、打破距離並邀觀者參與的『作品』為代表例。

其背景是當代藝術的課題從『什麼是藝術』（what is art）轉向『哪裡是藝術』（where is art）：藝術從美術館的白盒子走入『生活場域』，創作者從藝術家轉為『觀眾社群』，作品本身成為可被參與的『事件』；藝術的主體也進入『互為主體』（intersubjectivity），把社會、文化、性別、社區等議題帶入其中。源流上可溯及達達與杜象消弭藝術與生活的界線，以及波伊斯（Joseph Beuys）的『社會雕塑』（social sculpture）與『人人都是藝術家』；台灣則與 2002 年修正《文化藝術獎助條例》、公共藝術設置與『公民美學』的推動相關。

與『社會參與式藝術』、『社區藝術』、『新類型公共藝術』、『對話性創作』等概念高度重疊；本平台多數有居民共創的案例都可視為其實踐。文章也提醒兩個風險：民眾可能只淪為藝術家完成作品的助手，或落入『為參與而參與』的迷思——過度強調形式上的互動，反而讓民眾回到被動接收的角色，形成藝術與觀者『互為被動』（interpassive）的弔詭。

Definition (English)

Definition: Art in which the audience's or public's intervention and interaction themselves constitute the work, turning viewers from onlookers into the artist's collaborators.

Per Cheng Lin-chia in Modern Art no.177, Participatory Art refers to art formed through public intervention and interaction. The critic Lori Waxman argues that once a work brings in audience participation, the audience are no longer onlookers but the artist's collaborators. The article takes Rirkrit Tiravanija's cooking-and-sharing work at the 1993 Venice Biennale (Aperto 93), which breaks distance and invites viewers in, as a signature example.

Its backdrop is a shift in the contemporary-art question from what is art to where is art: art moves out of the museum white cube into the space of life, the maker shifts from artist to an audience community, and the work becomes a participable event; the subject of art enters intersubjectivity, drawing social, cultural, gender and community issues into itself. Its lineage runs from Dada and Duchamp dissolving the line between art and life to Joseph Beuys's social sculpture and everyone-is-an-artist; in Taiwan it is tied to the 2002 revision of the Culture and Arts Reward Act, public-art commissioning and the promotion of citizen aesthetics.

It overlaps heavily with socially engaged art, community art, New Genre Public Art and Conversation Pieces; most of the platform cases with resident co-creation can be read as its practice. The article also warns of two risks: people may become mere helpers finishing the artist's work, or fall into participation for participation's sake - over-stressing formal interaction can push people back into a passive, receiving role, an interpassive paradox between art and viewer. This entry follows that article.
