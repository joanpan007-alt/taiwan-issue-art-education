名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

駐村 Artist residency

定義：藝術家在一段期間內進駐某地、就地進行研究與創作的機制。

說明

駐村指藝術家在一段期間內進駐某個地方，進行藝術研究與創作。以歌德學院（台北）的駐村計畫為例，藝術家可以駐村最多三個月，目的是進行藝術研究、創作新作品，並促成兩地的交流。

在議題本位的計畫裡，駐村常讓藝術家有時間認識地方與居民，再創作。平台標為駐村的案例，像補龍計畫（三位原住民青年藝術家駐村青埔）、亞當計畫。

學理來源

Definition (English)

Definition: An arrangement in which an artist stays in a place for a period to research and create there.

A residency has an artist stay in a place for a period to research and create. In the Goethe-Institut Taipei program, for example, artists can reside for up to three months to do artistic research, create new work and foster exchange between the two cities.

In issue-based projects, residencies often give artists time to get to know a place and its residents before creating. Cases the platform labels this way include the Bu Long Project (three young Indigenous artists in residence in Qingpu) and the ADAM Artists' Laboratory.
