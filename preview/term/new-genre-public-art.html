名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

新類型公共藝術 New Genre Public Art

定義：強調社區參與、以人為本、重視過程的公共藝術取向，一詞源自 1995 年《量繪形貌：新類型公共藝術》一書所彙整、七〇年代以來的藝術實踐。

說明

依呂佩怡〈轉向「藝術/社會」：社會參與藝術實踐研究〉的梳理，「新類型公共藝術」（New Genre Public Art）一詞來自 1995 年出版的《量繪形貌：新類型公共藝術》（Mapping the Terrain: New Genre Public Art）。該書彙整七十年代以來的藝術實踐，指出它們在基本概念上已與先前的藝術不同；呂文並引藝評家 Suzi Gablik 以「連結性美學」（Connective Aesthetics）稱呼現代主義、個人主義之後的藝術樣貌，背景則是九〇年代美國的相關脈絡，如 1993 年芝加哥的「文化行動」展演。

呂文把「新類型公共藝術」與「藝術介入」「社區／社群藝術」「參與式藝術」「社會參與式藝術」等詞並列，視為台灣九〇年代末以來指稱同一波藝術實踐的多種名稱；它們共同強調公共性、參與性、關係性、合作性與過程性，不一定生產物件式作品或展覽。

本平台只在來源本身使用這個詞時才標記，相關概念的細部區辨另見「參與式藝術」「社會參與式藝術」「社區藝術」等條目。本條目依呂佩怡該研究案整理，所引書名與人名均為該文所述。

另據李奎壁〈社會參與式藝術〉一文，Suzanne Lacy 曾提出依參與深度劃分的「同心圓」參與者模型，用以區分不同程度的參與者與觀眾；該文把「新類型公共藝術」放在偶發藝術、Fluxus、情境主義國際與英國社群藝術等脈絡中一併討論。

Definition (English)

Definition: An approach to public art that stresses community participation, people-centredness and process; the term comes from the 1995 book Mapping the Terrain: New Genre Public Art, which gathered art practices from the 1970s on.

Per Lu Pei-yi's study 'Turning to Art/Society', the term New Genre Public Art comes from the 1995 book Mapping the Terrain: New Genre Public Art, which gathered practices from the 1970s onward and argued they already differed from earlier art in basic concept. Lu also cites the critic Suzi Gablik, who called the post-modernist, post-individualist mode of art 'Connective Aesthetics'; the backdrop is the 1990s US context, such as Chicago's 1993 'Culture in Action' event.

Lu lists New Genre Public Art alongside 'artistic intervention', 'community art', 'participatory art' and 'socially engaged art' as several names in Taiwan, from the late 1990s on, for one and the same wave of practice; together they stress publicness, participation, relationality, collaboration and process, and do not necessarily produce object-works or exhibitions.

The platform tags this term only where a source itself uses it; for finer distinctions see Participatory Art, Socially engaged art and Community art. This entry follows Lu Pei-yi's research project; the book title and names are as given there.

Li Kuei-pi's essay 'Socially Engaged Art' adds that Suzanne Lacy proposed a 'concentric circles' model of participation, distinguishing participants and audiences by depth of involvement; the essay places New Genre Public Art alongside Happenings, Fluxus, the Situationist International and British community art.
