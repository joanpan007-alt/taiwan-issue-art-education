自然權利 Natural rights

不依賴特定政府或法律、被認為普遍且不可剝奪的權利，與由法律體系賦予的法定權利相對。

說明

英文維基百科〈Natural rights and legal rights〉的導言說，有些哲學家區分兩種權利：自然權利是不依賴任何特定文化或政府的法律與習俗、因此普遍、基本且不可剝奪的權利；法定權利則是由特定法律體系賦予，可以被法律修改、廢止或限制（維基百科為協作編輯的百科，僅作一般定義參考）。

Definition (English)

Rights considered universal and inalienable, not dependent on any particular government or law, as opposed to legal rights bestowed by a legal system.

The lead of the English Wikipedia article "Natural rights and legal rights" says some philosophers distinguish two types of rights: natural rights are those not dependent on the laws or customs of any particular culture or government, and so are universal, fundamental and inalienable; legal rights are bestowed by a given legal system and can be modified, repealed or restrained by human laws (Wikipedia is collaboratively edited and used here only as a general reference).

Among the platform's cases, reports on the 2026 Mattauw Land Art Festival (K32) use the phrase: a Commercial Times article says the exhibition at the Cigang Art Center covers themes from "watershed protection and rights of nature to the boundary between water and people", and the 2026 festival's official site lists a "natural rights initiative" strand and a "Rights of Nature Court" (with a school-outreach session). Neither the reports nor the site says which philosophical sense is meant or defines the initiative, so the platform does not define it for them.
