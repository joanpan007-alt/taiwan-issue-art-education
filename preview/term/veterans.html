榮民 Veterans (rongmin)

國軍退除役官兵的通稱；政策上由退輔會輔導，社會討論常談到老年照顧與身分認同。

說明

國軍退除役官兵輔導委員會的 109 年度施政計畫，把對榮民的服務分為就學、就業、就醫、就養與安養照顧、服務關懷等面向，其中關懷對象包括年長、獨居及弱勢榮民與遺眷，並推動榮家的長照服務與失智床位。計畫寫到預計安置公費就養榮民 3 萬 6,015 人，另有 19 萬 3,108 人支領退休俸或生活補助費；這兩個數字的對象不同，不能相加，文件也沒有列出榮民總人數。

在身分認同方面，政大廖元豪的國科會研究以法制切入，摘要指來自中國的榮民經歷不同時期的身分轉換，從「暫居到生根」、從「外人到國民」，英文摘要也提到部分榮民因本土化與去中國化的趨勢，感到自己被當成外人。這裡只讀到摘要，報告正文無法確認。

Definition (English)

A common term for retired and discharged military personnel; policy is handled by the Veterans Affairs Council, and public discussion often turns to elder care and identity.

The Veterans Affairs Council's 2020 administrative plan divides services for veterans into study, employment, medical care, residential and elder care, and welfare outreach; the people it names for outreach include elderly, living-alone and disadvantaged veterans and survivors, and it promotes long-term care and dementia beds in veterans' homes. It plans to house 36,015 veterans on fully public-funded residential care and also mentions 193,108 people receiving retirement pay or living allowances; the two figures cover different groups and cannot be added, and the document does not give the total number of veterans.

On identity, a research report by Liao Yuan-hao at National Chengchi University approaches it through law; its abstract says veterans from China went through changing identities, from "temporary residents to rooted settlers" and "from outsiders to nationals", and the English abstract notes that some felt treated like aliens amid localization and de-sinicization. Only the abstract was read; the report body could not be confirmed.
