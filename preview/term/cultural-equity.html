文化平權（藝文平權） Cultural equity

保障人人平等參與文化的權利，不因身分、地域、年齡等條件而有差異。

說明

文化部的《推廣文化平權補助作業要點》第一點寫明目的是「推動、保障公眾平等之文化參與及文化近用權，且確保國人不因特定身分、社會性/別、身心狀況、年齡、地域、族群及其他條件等原因產生差異」。這是最接近定義的條文，要點本身沒有另外下定義。

「藝術下鄉」的相關說法也會用到這個詞，例如臺東縣政府文化處說明，活動是為落實縣長提倡的藝文平權、拉近城鄉文化距離，但那份來源是縣府廣編稿。要點檔案是台中市政府網站轉載的 PDF，不是文化部原站。

學理來源

  1. 文化部推廣文化平權補助作業要點（106 年訂定，110 年修正；檔案由台中市政府網站轉載，非文化部原站）
  2. 【幸福慢經濟】落實文化平權的「藝術下鄉」在臺東轉個彎就能遇見藝術（天下整合傳播部，2022-07-06；文末標示「臺東縣政府 廣告」，廣編稿）

相關名詞

藝術下鄉／藝術進鄉藝術推廣

Definition (English)

Guaranteeing everyone equal participation in culture, without differences by status, region, age or other conditions.

Article 1 of the Ministry of Culture's guidelines on subsidies for promoting cultural equity states its purpose as promoting and guaranteeing equal cultural participation and access, "ensuring people are not treated differently because of particular status, social or gender position, physical or mental condition, age, region, ethnicity or other conditions." This is the closest thing to a definition; the guidelines themselves do not define the term separately.

The term also appears around "art to the countryside": for example, Taitung County's Cultural Affairs Department says its activities put into practice the county magistrate's "equal access to arts and culture" and narrow the urban-rural gap, but that source is a county-government advertorial. The guidelines file is a PDF reposted on a Taichung City Government site, not on the Ministry's own.
