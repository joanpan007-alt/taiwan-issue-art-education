名詞解釋 ／ 創作與研究方法

影像發聲 Photovoice

讓參與者用拍照記錄自己的生活，再一起討論並對外發聲的方法。

說明

Photovoice 是一種參與式質性研究方法，由 Caroline Wang 與 Mary Ann Burris 於 1997 年正式提出。參與者針對主題拍攝自己的生活，再以團體討論挑選照片、寫說明，最後以展覽或報告呈現，目的是讓社群記錄並反思自身的優勢與關切，並把成果傳達給政策制定者。

週日限定風景移工國際攝影獎以 Photovoice 理論為基礎；由拍攝者決定拍什麼、希望外界怎麼認識自己。

Definition (English)

A method where participants photograph their own lives, then discuss and speak out together.

Photovoice is a participatory qualitative research method formally proposed by Caroline Wang and Mary Ann Burris in 1997. Participants photograph their lives around a theme, choose photos and write captions through group discussion, and present results as an exhibition or report. The aim is to let communities record and reflect on their strengths and concerns and convey them to policy makers.

Per the report, the Sundays Unseen migrant photography award is based on Photovoice theory; the photographers decide what to shoot and how they want outsiders to understand them.
