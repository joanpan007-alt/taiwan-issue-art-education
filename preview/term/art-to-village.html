藝術下鄉／藝術進鄉 Art to the countryside

把藝術活動帶到鄉鎮與偏鄉。「藝術下鄉」常見於縣市政府的巡迴展演，「藝術進鄉」是南藝大一項計畫的名稱。

說明

「藝術下鄉」在地方政府的巡迴展演中仍在使用。例如苗栗縣政府自 108 學年度起辦理「藝術下鄉」，以學生為主、跨校合作，2026 年首場在苑裡國小舉行，有 11 所學校師生演出，也邀請社區與家長參與（中時新聞網報導）。國藝會補助成果檔案庫也有以「藝術下鄉」為名的計畫，例如台灣芭蕾舞團 108 年（2019）在台東縣多所國中小進行示範教學與演出，目標是縮短城鄉的藝文差距，並讓民眾知道芭蕾不一定要進劇院才能欣賞。臺東縣政府文化處也以「藝術下鄉」為名辦理系列活動，包括在民宅庭院舉辦的「藝起來我家」、「表演藝術下鄉」與「行動美術館」等；文化處長說明，這是為落實縣長提倡的藝文平權、拉近城鄉文化距離，並提供在地藝文團隊表演平台（天下雜誌微笑台灣 2022 年的贊助內容，文末標示「臺東縣政府 廣告」，屬縣府觀點的廣編稿，不能當作獨立來源）。

「藝術進鄉」目前只查到是計畫名稱，不是通用詞。依南藝大電子報，它是《藝務新貌：老舊偏鄉好藝術之創新與翻轉計畫》的一部分，隸屬教育部高教深耕計畫「善盡社會責任」主軸，由藝術史學系許遠達老師授課。在官田有妖怪中，大學生帶官田國中生認識埤塘傳說、繪製角色，再放進展覽。

平台提醒：本次查閱的來源都沒有給出「藝術下鄉」的正式定義，也沒有說明它和「藝術進鄉」的差別（臺東縣文化處的說法只交代該縣活動的理念）；兩個詞哪個更常用、語感是否不同，目前沒有來源可證實，這裡不下結論。學界較常用的是「藝術介入」，張晴文指出台灣所說的「介入」不完全等同於社會參與式藝術。

Definition (English)

Bringing art activities to townships and rural areas. "Yishu xiaxiang" is common in county-government touring programmes; "Yishu jinxiang" is the name of a project at Tainan National University of the Arts.

"Yishu xiaxiang" (art to the countryside) is still used in local-government touring programmes. For example, Miaoli County Government has run it since the 2019 school year, mainly with students and cross-school cooperation; in 2026 the first session was held at Yuanli Elementary School, with teachers and students from 11 schools performing and the community and parents invited (China Times report). The National Culture and Arts Foundation's grant archive also lists projects named "Yishu xiaxiang"; for example, in 2019 (ROC year 108) Taiwan Ballet Company gave demonstration lessons and performances at several elementary and junior high schools in Taitung County, aiming to narrow the urban–rural arts gap and to show that ballet need not be seen only in a theatre. Taitung County Government's Cultural Affairs Department also runs a series called "Yishu xiaxiang", including courtyard sharing sessions "Yi Qi Lai Wo Jia", performing-arts tours and a mobile art museum; its director says the aim is to realize the arts-equity policy of the county magistrate, narrow the urban–rural cultural gap and give local groups a stage (a 2022 sponsored feature on Commonwealth Magazine's Smile Taiwan, marked at the end "Taitung County Government advertisement", i.e. an advertorial from the county's viewpoint, not an independent source).

"Yishu jinxiang" (Art Goes to the Countryside) has so far been found only as a project name, not a general term. According to the Tainan National University of the Arts e-newsletter, it is part of the "Innovation and Reversal of Good Art in Old Rural Areas" project, under the "social responsibility" strand of the Ministry of Education's Higher Education Sprout Project, taught by Hsu Yuan-ta of the art history department. In Guantian Has Monsters, university students led Guantian Junior High students to learn the pond legends and draw characters, which were then placed in an exhibition.

Note from the platform: none of the sources consulted gives a formal definition of "xiaxiang" or explains how it differs from "jinxiang" (the Taitung department's account only states the rationale of its own programme); which is more common and whether they carry different connotations cannot be confirmed from sources, so no conclusion is drawn here. Scholarship more often uses "art intervention"; Chang Ching-wen notes that Taiwan's "intervention" is not completely equivalent to socially engaged practice.
