埤塘 Irrigation ponds (pitang)

過去用來蓄水灌溉的池塘，桃園與台南官田一帶特別多。

說明

埤塘是過去用於農業灌溉的池塘。桃園有「千塘之鄉」之稱，歷史記載約有八千八百多口，一個世紀後只剩約兩千八百多口；石門水庫興建後，多數埤塘失去灌溉功能。部分埤塘改為兼具滯洪的生態公園，但也有研究者質疑過度水泥化。

南藝大電子報提到，官田的埤塘有灌溉、防災與養殖功能。官田有妖怪、補龍計畫都以埤塘為主題。

Definition (English)

Ponds once used to store water for irrigation; especially numerous in Taoyuan and Guantian, Tainan.

Ponds (pitang) were used for agricultural irrigation. According to PTS News, Taoyuan is known as the "land of a thousand ponds": records mention over 8,800, but a century later only about 2,800 remain, and after the Shimen Reservoir was built most lost their irrigation function. Some became ecological parks that also detain floodwater, though a researcher questions excessive concreting.

The Tainan National University of the Arts e-newsletter notes that Guantian's ponds serve irrigation, disaster-prevention and aquaculture functions. Guantian Has Monsters and the Bu Long Project both take ponds as a theme.
