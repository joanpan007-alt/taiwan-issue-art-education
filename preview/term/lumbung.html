Lumbung（共享穀倉） Lumbung

源自印尼的概念：社群把收成集中保存，再依共同決定分配。

說明

lumbung 直譯為「穀倉」，源自印尼農村的集體建築形式：社群把收成集中儲存，依共同決定的標準分配，作為未來的共同資源。documenta fifteen 由印尼集體 ruangrupa 擔任藝術總監，以此為實踐起點，核心原則包括集體性、資源累積與公平分配。

潮濕的複寫本（基隆）採 Lumbung 的方法，把地方文化資料與田野經驗分享給創作者。

《潮濕的複寫本》街區藝術行動

Definition (English)

An Indonesian concept: a community pools its harvest and distributes it by shared decision.

Lumbung literally means "rice barn," from the collective buildings of Indonesian villages where a community stores its harvest and distributes it by commonly agreed standards as a shared resource for the future. documenta fifteen, whose artistic direction was held by the Indonesian collective ruangrupa, took this as its starting point; its core principles include collectivity, resource accumulation and fair distribution.

The report on Damp Palimpsest (Keelung) says the team used the Lumbung approach, sharing local cultural material and field experience with the creators.
