名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

社區劇場 Community theatre

定義：由居民自己參與、以集體即興發展、不以專業演出為目的的戲劇形式。

說明

賴淑雅（台灣應用劇場發展中心）在《美育》第 188 期的文章指出，台灣的「社區劇場」為避開「被壓迫者」「改革」等字眼引起的排斥，以較中性的名稱推展被壓迫者劇場（源自巴西 Augusto Boal 的設計）；工作坊不教演出，而是引導民眾表達自己的故事和議題、與民眾互動對話，與強調完整製作、專業演出的劇場不同。

平台案例中，潮芳苑的人劇團社區劇場（K41）以田調與集體即興，讓芳苑在地素人把漁村生活編成戲，是清楚的例子。

學理來源

  1. 賴淑雅，〈是的，我們在從事被壓迫者劇場工作！〉，《美育》第188期（約 2011–2012）

Definition (English)

Definition: A form of theatre taken part in by residents themselves, developed through collective improvisation and not aimed at professional performance.

Lai Shu-ya (Taiwan Centre for Applied Theatre) writes in Arts Education no. 188 that Taiwan’s ‘community theatre’ used a more neutral name to carry the Theatre of the Oppressed (from Brazil’s Augusto Boal), avoiding the pushback that words like ‘oppressed’ or ‘reform’ provoked; workshops do not teach performance but guide people to express their own stories and issues in dialogue, unlike theatre focused on a finished professional production.

Among the platform’s cases, Ren Theatre’s Fangyuan community theatre (K41) uses fieldwork and collective improvisation so local non-professionals turn fishing-village life into theatre.
