眷村 Military dependents' village

國軍及眷屬聚居的社區；許多老舊眷村已依條例改建，近年也以文化保存與活化的形式被重新看見。

說明

國軍老舊眷村改建條例第 1 條說明，立法目的包括加速更新國軍老舊眷村、興建住宅照顧原眷戶及中低收入戶，以及「保存眷村文化」（民國 96 年修正公報）。可見眷村同時是被改建的老舊社區，也被視為需要保存的文化。

眷村的基本定義在這裡是編輯整理，沒有直接引用定義條文。平台案例中，侯淑姿《高雄眷村三部曲》（K08）以居民口述記錄眷村改建與拆遷中的生活與離散記憶，眾人成莊（K44）、二村開門（K45）則把眷村遺構轉化為創作與展演空間。

Definition (English)

Neighborhoods where military personnel and their families lived; many old ones have been rebuilt under a special act.

Article 1 of the Act for Rebuilding Old Military Dependents' Villages states its purposes, including speeding up the renewal of old villages, building housing for original residents and low- and middle-income households, and "preserving military dependents' village culture" (2007 amendment gazette). So these villages are both old neighborhoods to be rebuilt and a culture seen as worth preserving.

The basic definition here is an editorial summary, not a quoted legal definition. Hou Shu-tzu's Kaohsiung trilogy (K08) uses residents' oral accounts to record life and memories of displacement during rebuilding and demolition, while Making the Village Together (K44) and Second Village Opens (K45) turn village remains into spaces for creation and performance.
