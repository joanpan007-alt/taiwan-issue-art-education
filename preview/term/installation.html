裝置（裝置藝術） Installation art

大型、混合媒材的作品，常為特定地點或短暫時間而作，整個空間就是作品。

說明

Tate 的藝術名詞表把裝置藝術描述為「large-scale, mixed-media constructions, often designed for a specific place or for a temporary period of time」，也就是大型、混合媒材、常為特定地點或限定時間而作的作品。Tate 還說，它是一個完整統一的體驗，而不是一組獨立作品的陳列，作品有時也被稱為「環境」。

平台的「藝術形式」篩選裡，「裝置」是最常見的形式之一，案例多半是在社區空間、公共空間或展間裡，用現成物、回收材料或居民提供的物件做成。「裝置」在這裡只是形式標籤，不代表作品有居民參與。

Definition (English)

Large-scale, mixed-media work, often made for a specific place or time, in which the whole space is the work.

Tate's art terms describe installation art as "large-scale, mixed-media constructions, often designed for a specific place or for a temporary period of time." Tate adds that it is a complete unified experience rather than a display of separate artworks, and that such works are sometimes called "environments."

In the platform's "art form" filter, installation is one of the most common forms; cases are mostly made in community or public spaces or galleries from found objects, recycled materials or objects residents provide. Here it is only a form label and does not mean residents took part in making the work.
