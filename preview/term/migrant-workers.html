移工 Migrant workers

來台從事產業或看護等工作的外籍工作者；政府統計分為產業與社福兩類。

說明

勞動部統計把外籍工作者分為產業移工與社福移工。以 2020 年底為例，總數約 70.9 萬人，其中產業 45.7 萬、社福 25.2 萬；依國籍，印尼最多（26.3 萬），其次為越南（23.7 萬）。這是 2020 年的資料，現況會不同。

平台有多個以移工為參與者的案例，像週日限定風景（移工攝影獎）、共生窗景詩（桃園後站）。

Definition (English)

Foreign workers who come to Taiwan for industrial or caregiving work; official statistics split them into industrial and social-welfare categories.

The Ministry of Labor splits foreign workers into industrial and social-welfare migrant workers. At the end of 2020 the total was about 709,000: 457,000 industrial and 252,000 social-welfare; by nationality, Indonesians were the largest group (263,000), followed by Vietnamese (237,000). These are 2020 figures, and the situation today differs.

The platform has several cases with migrant workers as participants, such as Sundays Unseen (the migrant photography award) and Symbiotic Window Poem (Taoyuan's rear station).
