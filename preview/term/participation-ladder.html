名詞解釋 ／ 藝術實踐的類型

居民參與（參與的層次） Resident participation (levels of participation)

定義：描述居民在計畫中參與程度高低的概念；從被告知、被諮詢到共同決定，Arnstein 的「參與階梯」是最常被引用的分類。

說明

Sherry Arnstein 在 1969 年的〈A Ladder of Citizen Participation〉（《Journal of the American Planning Association》第 35 卷第 4 期）提出八級階梯：1 操弄（manipulation）、2 治療（therapy）屬於「非參與」；3 告知（informing）、4 諮詢（consultation）、5 安撫（placation）屬於「形式參與」；6 夥伴關係（partnership）、7 權力授與（delegated power）、8 市民控制（citizen control）屬於「市民權力」。

平台的案例常寫「居民參與」，但來源很少說明參與到哪一級：是被訪談、參加工作坊，還是參與決定？所以平台沒有替案例套用這個階梯，案例頁的「居民如何參與」是照來源原文整理。這個階梯原本討論的是都市規劃與公共決策，延伸到藝術計畫是平台的類比，並非原文用法；本詞條讀到的是一份地方政府網站轉載的階梯摘要，不是 1969 年原文。

Definition (English)

Definition: A concept describing how much residents actually take part in a project, from being informed or consulted to sharing decisions; Arnstein's 'ladder of participation' is the most-cited model.

Sherry Arnstein's 1969 article "A Ladder of Citizen Participation" (Journal of the American Planning Association 35(4)) proposes eight rungs: 1 manipulation and 2 therapy (nonparticipation); 3 informing, 4 consultation and 5 placation (tokenism); 6 partnership, 7 delegated power and 8 citizen control (citizen power).

Cases often say "resident participation" without saying at which rung. The platform therefore does not assign rungs to cases; "how residents took part" follows each source's wording. The ladder was written about urban planning and public decisions, so applying it to art projects is the platform's analogy. This entry draws on a summary reproduced on a local-government site, not the 1969 original.
