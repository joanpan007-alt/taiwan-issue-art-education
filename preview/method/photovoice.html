教學方法 ／ 方法

Photovoice／影像自述

把相機交給參與者，由他們決定拍什麼、希望外界怎麼認識自己。

怎麼帶

  1. 給一個開放的提問（例如「如果每月只有一天假」）
  2. 參與者自行拍攝，不規定主題
  3. 小組一起討論選片與下標題
  4. 展出時由拍攝者決定呈現與說法

適用

移工、少年、常被貼標籤的群體；核心是把話語權交給當事人。

使用此方法的案例（3）

《週日限定風景》移工國際攝影獎特展逆風計畫（誠正中學）原型樂園（Prototype Paradise）

Method (English)

Photovoice

Hand the camera to participants and let them decide what to shoot and how they want to be seen.

  1. Pose one open question (e.g. ‘if you had one day off a month’)
  2. Participants shoot on their own, with no set subject
  3. Choose photos and write captions together in small groups
  4. At the show, the photographer decides how it is presented

Migrant workers, youth, often-labelled groups; the point is to hand narrative control to the people themselves.
