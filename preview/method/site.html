教學方法 ／ 方法

在歷史現場與軍事遺構裡創作

不把歷史現場當背景看板，而是直接在防空洞、碉堡、眷村、老屋裡創作或展演，讓空間本身成為教材與媒材；這是限地製作（site-specific）用在記憶場域教學的一種應用。

怎麼帶

  1. 選一處有張力、仍保有痕跡的現場（碉堡、防空洞、眷村、廢墟）
  2. 先帶參與者用身體與感官認識這個空間：走、聽、觸、記錄
  3. 從空間的用途與歷史拋問，而不是先講解牌式地給答案
  4. 讓作品或展演留在原地發生，而非搬到展間；必要時結合在地媒材

適用

地方史、冷戰與戰地記憶、文資活化；把抽象歷史變成可被身體經驗的現場。

Method (English)

Working inside historical sites and military remains

Don’t treat the historical site as a backdrop; create or perform straight inside bomb shelters, bunkers, villages and old houses, so the space itself becomes both lesson and material; it is one educational application of site-specific art to memory-laden places.

  1. Pick a charged site that still bears its traces (bunker, bomb shelter, village, ruin)
  2. First have participants know the space through body and senses: walk, listen, touch, record
  3. Pose questions from the site’s use and history, rather than giving wall-label answers
  4. Let the work or performance happen in place, not moved to a gallery; bring in local materials where it fits

Local history, Cold-War and battlefield memory, heritage activation; turns abstract history into something the body can experience on site.
