教學方法 ／ 方法

在地素材與廢棄物再製

用在地或被丟棄的材料（漁網、漂流木、海廢、舊布條）創作，讓材料本身承載議題。

怎麼帶

  1. 一起蒐集材料：淨灘、撿拾或向社區徵集
  2. 認識材料的來歷與它背後的問題
  3. 動手改造成作品
  4. 展示時連結材料的來源故事

適用

環境、社區；讓行動（淨灘）與創作合而為一。

Method (English)

Local & found-material making

Make work from local or discarded materials — fishing nets, driftwood, marine debris, old banners — so the material itself carries the issue.

  1. Gather materials together: beach cleanups, scavenging, or a community call
  2. Learn where the material came from and the problem behind it
  3. Remake it into a work by hand
  4. Link the display back to the material’s origin story

Environment, community; fuses action (a cleanup) with creation.
