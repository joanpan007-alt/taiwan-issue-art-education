教學方法 ／ 方法

在地職人與耆老當老師：以活工藝共作

刻意翻轉師生角色，請社區裡的匠師與耆老當老師，帶參與者以在地仍活著的工藝技藝（編織、染、陶、糊紙等）共同創作。

怎麼帶

  1. 先在社區裡找到還在做的匠師或耆老，請他們當老師
  2. 由職人示範並帶領在地技藝（編織、植物染、陶、糊紙等）
  3. 參與者動手學，再一起把技藝用到共同作品上
  4. 把成果連回這項技藝的來歷與傳承脈絡

適用

原住民族與客庄文化、傳統工藝活化；讓在地知識被看見、也被傳下去。（與『在地素材再製』『跨世代共作』部分重疊，差別在這裡以『職人為師、活的技藝』為核心。）

使用此方法的案例（5）

牡丹．森里海環境藝術設計營藝術轉動社區（金門、澎湖）藝術域承—社區故事共創計畫《藝耆共舞．客庄日常》交會地方・藝駐共創（臺東藝術駐地共創計畫）《裹山》Dungku Asang（林介文／Labay Eyong）

Method (English)

Local masters and elders as teachers

Deliberately invert the teacher–student roles: invite the community’s craftspeople and elders to teach, and co-create with participants through living local crafts (weaving, dyeing, ceramics, paper-craft, etc.).

  1. Find craftspeople or elders in the community who still practise, and invite them to teach
  2. Have the masters demonstrate and lead a living local craft (weaving, plant-dyeing, ceramics, paper-craft…)
  3. Participants learn hands-on, then put the craft to work on a shared piece
  4. Tie the result back to the craft’s origins and its line of transmission

Indigenous and Hakka cultures, reviving traditional crafts; lets local knowledge be seen and passed on. (Overlaps partly with found-material making and cross-generational co-creation; the difference is its focus on masters-as-teachers and living skills.)
