計畫簡介

「酷共生」是 2023 年 7 月 22 日至 9 月 17 日在北師美術館的性別議題展。

11 組藝術家與科學家等行動者合作，用生物與性別多樣性的故事，讓觀眾認識並接納性少數。

報導指展覽希望觀眾藉此認識生物與性別的多元面向。

Summary (English)

"Cool Symbiosis" Gender Exhibition

“Cool Symbiosis” is a gender-themed exhibition at the MoNTUE (Museum of National Taipei University of Education) from 22 July to 17 September 2023.

Eleven groups of artists worked with scientists and other actors, using stories of biodiversity and gender diversity to help visitors understand and accept sexual minorities.

The report says the show hopes visitors will come to know the many dimensions of biology and gender.
