《潮濕的複寫本》街區藝術行動

國立臺灣海洋大學人文創新與社會實踐計畫（HISP）、曙・初見咖啡「藝文委託行」；青年創作者　已查證

計畫簡介

《潮濕的複寫本》是 2026 年 8 月 22 日至 9 月 20 日在基隆仁愛區至善大樓公共空間與初見咖啡館舉辦的街區藝術行動。

青年創作者走進崁仔頂與委託行街區取材，團隊採 Lumbung（共享穀倉）方法，把地方文化資料與田野經驗分享給創作者，並與大樓管委會協調空間。

作品展於大樓公共空間改成的社區藝廊，描寫基隆舊城的縫補業、魚市與鐵道記憶。

Summary (English)

Damp Palimpsest: Neighborhood Art Action

Damp Palimpsest is a neighbourhood art action held from 22 August to 20 September 2026 in the public spaces of the Zhishan Building in Ren’ai District, Keelung, and at Chujian Café.

Young creators went to the Kanzaiding and consignment-shop (weituohang) districts to gather material; the team used the Lumbung (shared rice barn) method, sharing local cultural materials and fieldwork experience with the creators, and coordinated the space with the building’s management committee.

The works were shown in a community gallery converted from the building’s public space, depicting the sewing trade, fish market and railway memories of Keelung’s old town.
