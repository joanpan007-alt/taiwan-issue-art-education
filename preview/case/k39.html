教育 展覽 繪畫 藝術教育 策展與展覽 青少年

計畫簡介

「聽見上河的聲音」是國立故宮博物院非典型教育展，2019 年 12 月 20 日至 29 日在新竹縣誠正中學行政大樓 3 樓禮堂舉行。故宮自 1990 年代起以巡迴展服務收容人，2010 年起與矯正機關合作複製文物展示，2012 年起合作規劃藝術課程、培訓青少年收容人導覽。

這次故宮教育團隊與誠正中學校內國文、數學、汽機車修護與電腦職訓課程教師合作，將【清 院本 清明上河圖】融入教學；並從【宋 蘇軾 書黃州寒食詩】連結學生生命經驗、延伸當代饒舌韻腳，學生自己作詞作曲。籌備期間花了將近 9 個月教導學生相關技能。

展場有故宮文物複製品、VR 與影片，也展出學生以粉彩、漫畫、禪繞畫、LINE 貼圖、雕塑、動力自造與音樂詞曲創作的作品，學生並擔任導覽解說，少年的全名與容貌不顯影，以音樂、口述與文字表達心聲。

來源怎麼說

教育借鏡

把故宮文物（清明上河圖、寒食帖）當起點，讓矯正學校學生用粉彩、漫畫、貼圖等多種媒材重新詮釋，還自己當導覽，示範了「用高門檻的經典文物，做低門檻的親近設計」。要提醒的是：報導像新聞稿，學生如何參與創作沒有細寫。

Summary (English)

"Hearing the Voices of Along the River" Atypical Education Exhibition, National Palace Museum at Chengcheng Junior High School

“Hearing the Sound of Along the River” is the National Palace Museum’s atypical education exhibition, held from 20 to 29 December 2019 in the auditorium on the 3rd floor of the administration building of Cheng Cheng Junior High School in Hsinchu County. Since the 1990s the Museum has served inmates with touring exhibitions, since 2010 has worked with correctional institutions on replica displays, and since 2012 on art courses and training young inmates as guides.

This time the Museum’s education team worked with the school’s Chinese, mathematics, auto repair and computer vocational teachers to bring Along the River During the Qingming Festival (Qing court version) into teaching; and from Su Shi’s Calligraphy on the Cold Food Observance poem in Huangzhou it linked to students’ life experience and extended to contemporary rap rhymes, with students writing their own lyrics and music. Preparation took nearly nine months of teaching students the relevant skills.

The venue had replicas of Palace Museum objects, VR and film, and works by students in pastel, comics, Zentangle, LINE stickers, sculpture, mechanical making and music and lyrics; students also served as guides. The youths’ full names and faces are not shown, and they express themselves through music, spoken words and text.
