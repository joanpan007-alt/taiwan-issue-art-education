「畫，25」九二一震後埔里展

計畫簡介

「畫，25」是九二一地震 25 週年的埔里展，2024 年 9 月 12 日記者會，展至 9 月 30 日；計畫由新故鄉文教基金會主辦，文化部支持規劃。

11 位畫家回到埔里受災地創作，以繪畫呈現新舊地貌對比，並搭配老畫家的震後素描。

原作展於紙教堂「流」藝廊，戶外複製展於埔里聯合行政園區與集集火車站前廣場。

Summary (English)

"Painting, 25": Puli After the 921 Earthquake

“Draw, 25” is a Puli exhibition marking the 25th anniversary of the 921 earthquake, with a press conference on 12 September 2024 and the show running to 30 September; it was organised by the New Homeland Foundation with Ministry of Culture support in planning.

Eleven painters returned to the quake-hit area of Puli to work, using paintings to show the contrast between old and new landscapes, alongside an older painter’s post-quake sketches.

The originals were shown at the “Flow” gallery of the Paper Dome, with outdoor reproductions at the Puli Joint Administrative Park and the plaza in front of Jiji Railway Station.
