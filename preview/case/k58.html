口湖濱海藝術季（海大 USR 團隊進駐）

國立臺灣海洋大學 USR 團隊；沁窟ㄚ腳文創協會（理事長王耀新）；口湖鄉公所　待查證

計畫簡介

沁窟ㄚ腳文創協會長期在雲林縣口湖鄉下崙地區推動社區營造與藝術教育。2025 年的口湖濱海藝術季由國立臺灣海洋大學（海大）USR 團隊與該協會、口湖鄉公所合作舉辦。

藝術季認養一座 2025 桃園臺灣燈會結束後的花燈，點燈儀式由鄉長主持，並以海廢藝術展示讓居民在賞燈的同時理解環境永續，呼應花燈二次利用與海廢再利用的理念。

居民攜家帶眷參與，但共創過程描述不足，居民參與證據弱到中；藝術季確切日期與名稱、海廢展示作品由誰創作，來源未載。

來源怎麼說

1111 新聞（海大校方供稿）寫到，藝術季由海大 USR 團隊與沁窟ㄚ腳文創協會、口湖鄉公所合作，認養一座 2025 桃園臺灣燈會結束後的花燈，點燈儀式由鄉長主持，並以海廢藝術展示推廣海洋教育。

Summary (English)

Kouhu Seaside Arts Festival (with the NTOU USR team in residence)

The Chenku Yajiao Cultural and Creative Association has long promoted community building and arts education in the Xialun area of Kouhu, Yunlin. The 2025 Kouhu Coastal Art Season was held by the National Taiwan Ocean University (NTOU) USR team together with the association and the Kouhu Township Office.

The art season adopted a lantern left over from the 2025 Taoyuan Taiwan Lantern Festival, with the lighting ceremony led by the township mayor, and used an ocean-debris art display to let residents understand environmental sustainability while enjoying the lantern, echoing the ideas of reusing lanterns and reusing marine debris.

Residents came with their families, but the co-creation process is thinly described, so evidence of resident participation is weak to moderate; the exact dates and name of the art season and who made the marine-debris works are not given in the sources.
